Magic is a concept that's used quite often in [[KTE]], however it has remained ill-defined until now. There are two main categories to magic, that being Fundamental Magic and Compound Magic. It's sometimes the lifeline of species, or a good thing to have to ward off and kill [[Malice]].

Most types of fundamental magic aren't interesting, and are their respective things but woven with Magic, giving it more usefulness but not changing the properties. Fire Magic, as an example, is analogous to Fire, just casted with Magic in some manner, considering Magic is its own underlying thing.

## State

Magic can also have two states, that being Crude and Refined. All types of magic can be crude or refined, and Malice takes more damage from crude magic, although it's much harder to reliably keep in its crude state *and* do attacks with.

If an attack uses magic, it most often uses refined magic to work. It's easy to use, but a lot of its raw potential is lost in the exchange of convenience.

## Composition

Sometimes, however, Magic can be moved from one form to another, or even stitched together with other kinds of magic, to form Compound Magic, and each compound magic has an Order to it. Notably, there's no limit to what types of magic can go together, and no limit on the amount of further compositions.

- First Order magic is fundamental, which is what most magic types are going to be based from. Many of these will not be listed.
- Second Order magic is the first compound set, which is based exclusively from First Order magic.
- Third Order magic are compound magic types which are formed from first and second order magic.

### Limitations

- Every composition must involve at least one First Order type in every step of the process.
- If a composition isn't valid, [[Inert]] is the formed magic type. In short, it doesn't do anything and cannot be decomposed without extra effort.
