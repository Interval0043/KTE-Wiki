A [[Magic]] composition of [[Fire]] and [[Metal]], in which the attacks deployed take on a lot of Metal's characteristics, but being significantly more resilient and having some new qualities.

## Properties

### Armor-Piercing

Due to its stronger nature, Wrought magic excels at breaking through most types of armor. It still has plenty of damage against flesh and other things, but armor or anything metallic will be punched right through if it isn't fortified well enough.

### Superpositional Conductivity

Wrought's interesting nature lends it to being both a conductor and an insulator at will, allowing it to work in tandem with [[Electric]], or contradict it. However, this cannot be managed, and is left up to the wrought's decision, oftentimes doing a mix of both.

### Heft

However, because of an increase in strength and otherwise, anything enhanced with Wrought magic will nearly double in weight, oftentimes encumbering the holder if they aren't careful. While this does lend itself to much harder hits, and especially its Armor-Piercing property, it can also make it significantly slower to move and manage.
