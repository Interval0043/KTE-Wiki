Souls come in many different interpretations, and different types within said interpretation. No one way is correct, and some species have it differently too.

## [[Psychotropicz, Crazer|Crazer]]'s Interpretation

This involves [[Sprinklekit|Sprinklekits]] and how their souls are used.

>[!abstract]
>To be precise, there are 3 categories: emotion-driven and non-emotional. It depends on what exactly the soul is based from, for what category it falls into. For those based on emotions (e.g. Wrath with [[Jacob Will]]), they are considered Emotion-Driven. For those not based on emotions, they are considered Non-Emotional. Some of the [[Astral Family]] have Hybrid souls, of which they are based on a mix of emotions and sometimes abstract concepts.

It's listed in the [[Sprinklekit]] species page under "Souls".

## [[Interval (Author)|Polytope]]'s Interpretation

While not fully feature-complete, as it cannot handle standard organs like other soul types do, it's used in the [[Hedron]] and [[Manifold]] species.

> [!abstract]
> There's really only one type of soul for the [[Hedron|Hedrons]] and [[Manifold|Manifolds]], and that's of their Binding Soul. For the Hedrons, it acts as the barrier and connection between their Shell and Gas, but for the Manifolds, it's what allows them to grab on to things.
> It also acts as their mental state. Damage to the soul could cause irreparable problems when not addressed quickly. As an example, [[Bass]]' soul damage causes his ADPD.
> While it could generate magic for them provided the entity is classified as [[Kingdom-Formed]], the main source of magic is still from the Core if it's a [[Hedron]].

They're listed in the [[Hedron]] and [[Manifold]] species pages under "Souls", or similar.
