BLIGHT is a form of magic that feasts upon the life-force of living beings; A mere fraction of Dire’s essence that strips away and eats everything but itself. Sourced and originated from Unstable Catalyst, but molded in the image of Licidea, The First. Kruula are the only species that actively seeks, replicates, and reaps of this hellish energy. For an individual to be a wielder of BLIGHT, they usually need to be a soulless entity, or possess certain characteristics, such as having broad desire like destroying objects, hurting others, or killing. It can also intertwine with deeply-rooted issues and mental illness/disorders.

BLIGHT alone is a rogue, volatile force that forces decay and weakness on whatever it comes in contact with; This includes souls, or any kind of being that tries to absorb or manipulate it by force. Just like MIGHT/Eden Energy, it gravitates to a host that can master its power. 

There are many properties to BLIGHT, some happen on their own, some don’t. However, the important thing about Dissonant Energy’s decay effect is that it can be controlled, and it isn’t just apart of the “rogue” state the magic is in. 

The user of BLIGHT can spread the decay effect by imbuing attacks with it, or letting it radiate off of their body.

What BLIGHT is renowned for is its use of giving a user access to the manipulation of souls. This process of mastering soul magic heavily varies with anyone, and all of its application are more than often than not learned. Essentially, the user “learns” to bend, create, and shape souls by simply putting their mind to it hard enough, constructing their own active and passive abilities.

### Side Effects

Do note that these effects can vary from person to person. These can increase and decrease in severity depending upon how serious of an effect BLIGHT has.

* Feeling of a bottomless appetite that which they can never fulfill for a given period of time; This feeling can be induced even in entities that don’t require sustenance to live. The feeling can induce pain in organs that are responsible for receiving this kind of information.

* Experience brief periods of confusion or emptiness, like they’re missing a piece of information that never existed. Will often drift off into long stretches of disorganized thoughts before finally recollecting theirselves. 

* Difficulty for their bodies to recover after sustaining physical injuries, as the regeneration process is either slowed or at a complete stop. Wounds will not clot and scab, and bruises formed will stay longer.

* Marks will form on the body that are in the shape of cracks, though usually form through scars.
