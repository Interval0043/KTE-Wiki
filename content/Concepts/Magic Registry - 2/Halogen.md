Not to be confused with the 17th group of the periodic table. Halogen is a [[Magic]] composition, being extraordinarily reactive and unstable.

> [!warning]
> Only for experienced users. This will often cause self-injury if mismanaged.

- 2-4% Metal
- 2-4% Earth
- 92-96% Fire

## Properties

Halogen is an interesting type to be explored.

### Intolerability

Halogen magic rarely reacts well with other magic types, opting instead to contradict them and continue being really unstable. Depending on the precise composition, this property will be increased or decreased.

### Persistence

Halogen does not decay easily, and attacks will linger for a while, attaching and bouncing off of surfaces erratically. Once an attack is fired, there's a lot more work cleaning it up than sending it off.

### Impurity

Halogen is much more often available as Crude magic, lending it to being significantly stronger against [[Malice]], but also a lot harder to set off and get going. When in its crude form, though, it appears much more as a sludge than actual magic. Be careful with it!
