## Origin

Created by the embodiment of Existence, [[Counter Will]], as means to deal with their own loneliness, and to do so, by sharing their essence to others, created a family related by blood, even if not by the traditional sense. They shared aspects similar to Counter Will, including resistance to aging, and other abilities not quite as relevant to what [[Counter Will|Counter]] wanted, but that served as means to protect the Family further.

## Description

They're a family acting as a safe haven for themselves and, when need arises, as a powerful political and destructive force. Although not as powerful as they used to be, due to the death of Counter Will, and before that, [[Noite Will]], they are still relatively powerful due to their ties to the [[Legion Of Nazareth|L.O.N.]].

## Hierarchy

The Hierarchy of the Will Family serves as titles, and as the pecking order within the family when it comes to important decisions and even minor situations such as who listens to who, or who decides which person does which chores. There are five rankings within this hierarchy, three of which are based on solely combat capabilities. When it comes to who's say holds the most weight, the hierarchy goes as following:

1. Elders
2. Class A Combatants
3. Class B Combatants = Class C Combatants
4. Non-Combatants

## Roles

### Elders

As the name implies, this title is held by the Elders of the family, a title gained through proving yourself consistently throughout a thousand years.
One may become an Elder if and only if all of the following criteria are met:

1. They are already part of the A Class.
2. They are 1000 Years old, or older.
3. The current Elders unanimously decide they are a good fit to become an Elder.

### Class A Combatants

The most powerful class of combatants within the Will Family, with the sole exception of the Elders. Due to their extended experience, they are allowed to have more of a say in important matters within the family.
One may reach Class A by meeting the following criteria:

1. Go through the "Trial of Worth" with the permission of at least one Elder.
2. Being a fully developed adult.

### Class B Combatants

Incredibly powerful members of the family, often those that are either too young or too inexperienced to become a Class A combatant.
The Criteria for Class B are as follows:

1. Being equal or above the members of Class C when it comes to combat prowess.
2. Having shown consistent competence for both battles and tasks alike.

### Class C Combatants

A catch-all title for the able-bodied combatants of the family.
To be considered a Class C combatant, one must meet the following criteria:

1. Be significantly stronger than any common civilian, and above the baseline strength and prowess of their species.
2. Be able-bodied enough to fight.

### Non-Combatants

Members of the family who are either too young or uninterested in being a combatant, or those who do not meet the criteria for Class C. In cases where a member of the Combatants, due to any circumstances, are not able bodied enough to fight, they'll be temporarily placed within this category, but will keep the authority of their old title, until recovery.

## Familial Aspects

The unique capabilities, powers and properties of the family members are caused by their connection to Counter Will. Combatants with higher skill and control over their body and magic have shown the ability to modify and evolve these abilities with enough effort.

### Harden

The [[Magic]] of Will Family members may have the unique property of self annihilation when conjured in a certain manner, creating a new "Type" of magic, and the technique "Harden" works by using said type as a thin veil around the user's body.
Self Annihilation causes magic to simply cease to be when foreign force is applied to it.
Upon impact, the force, or energy behind a physical blow will be mostly "Absorbed" by the user's magic. This works due as the magic that constitutes the veil functions as a physical layer, due to how much magic is compressed within it, and before the force can travel from said layer to the body of the user, the magic ceases to be alongside the energy within it, before said force can reach the user.

Of course, this requires extreme focus to pull off and keep up, and if not done correctly, will simply waste stamina and magic reserves as a veil too thick or too faint would dissipate almost instantly. Thus, the less experienced users will use this as a defense right before being hit, rather than all the time, alongside doing it in small scales, such as only around one's arms or forearms.

A weakness of this is the fact that upon being struck and absorbing the force of a strike, the veil of magic becomes unstable enough that it self-annihilates completely, instead of only the magic that holds the foreign force. This means, while Harden is incredibly useful against powerful, singular strikes, multiple, less significant strikes will render it useless.

### Unpaired Vision

Unlike what the name may suggest, this ability does not actually work in tandem with the user's sight, but instead acts as a sixth sense. While it has multiple versions that can be learned separately, they all fall under the Unpaired Vision aspect, and someone who masters all of them would become a real pain in the ass to fight against.

#### Intent & Emotion

The ability to sense the intent and emotions of those around you; Its precision, range and limitations are defined by your mastery over it.

#### Position & Matter

The ability to, regardless of the location or whether or not light is present, know how your surroundings look like. This comes with incredibly heightened spatial and depth perception. It can be pictured as a form of quiet, more advanced echolocation.

#### Magic & Reveal

The ability to sense and pinpoint the flow of magic in your surroundings. An advanced enough user may even use the way magic is wielded by the opponent as a manner to guess what exactly their ability does.

#### Future & Prediction

Precognition. The ability to see, albeit a very short time-frame, into the future. With the exception of Counter Will, the time-frame of this ability within the family ranges from 100 milliseconds to 8 seconds. It is the hardest of the four applications of Unpaired Vision to learn and master.

### Will Against Reality (W.A.R.)

An incredibly broad, all-encompassing ability that, albeit innate and constant in every member of the Will family, only two people in all of history were able to use it to it's fullest extent.
It is the resistance against reality warping of any kind, alongside the ability to mold reality itself. Usually, it manifests itself as simple things, such as fitting more objects than it would be normally possible within a container or pocket, but those who can use it's fullest potential are able to, quite literally, become nigh-omnipotent.
