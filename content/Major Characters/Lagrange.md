## History

Lagrange was introduced in [[Kingdom SE]] as the third [[Hedron]], the first two being [[Kepler]] and [[Poinsot]]. The town of [[Khazingrad]] wasn't too kind to him, much like his siblings, so he adapted to the situation and began learning how to control fire. Soon enough, though, he slipped into arsonism, keeping him in its clutches for a long while.

His siblings didn't help him much through his problems at the time, and as such Lagrange only began liking them late into Kingdom SE, nearly bordering [[Kingdom III]]. Soon enough, though, he'd forge a tight-knit bond with the two of them, even if he was still shackled by his problems.

Throughout [[TFCT]], Lagrange began to slowly peel away from his cravings, opting to resort to [[Signus]]' assistance in managing him. However, not much else came of it other than Lagrange doing his own thing for a while.

Entering [[Eternal Infection]], Signus has sent him on a mission to use his, now smaller, cravings for arson to kill [[Malice]], albeit under supervision.

## Appearance

Lagrange often is seen clearing small spots of Malice in a specific uniform, that being a highly flame-resistant coffee-colored overcoat with a hood optional and red accents, equally as resistant padded pants, a black undershirt, and black boots. The entirety of the uniform was given to him by Signus to make it obvious that he's doing a job and he's doing it safely.

When not clearing Malice, Lagrange often just wears a gray undershirt and shorts. Unlike his fellow Hedrons, he doesn't actively cover a lot of his body, considering he doesn't have much to hide. Visible in this state are his many protrusions, dents, and cracks on his shell as evidence of his prior struggles.

## Personality

Lagrange, even now, still has a lingering bitterness to his movements. While he remains a very calm person, he also holds grudges and doesn't hold words. He's been through enough in his life to where he doesn't hesitate to start something in the event he needs to. Quite often, he'll wait patiently for the other person to finish speaking before talking himself, as he believes that not being rude might salvage something about him.

Buried beneath the calm bitterness is an innate curiosity for the world, one he shares with Kepler, but he tends to not satiate it unless it's a particularly peaceful time, or right after a controlled burn.

He still struggles from the remnants of pyromania and chronic arsonist behavior, so occasionally you'll see him stare at flames or flick his lighter out of habit, trying to ease his own mind.

## Relationships

Other than his siblings, Lagrange hasn't talked to many people, and worked with fewer. The main issue is that his reputation often preceded him, making it difficult for him to reach out, as people knew him only as the "crazy arsonist".

## Weaponry

### Divine Lighter

This lighter was passed down to him by the deity of the Hedrons, [[Hallis]]. It is inside of a brushed metal case, fortified with a strong, unnamed compound, thought to have originated from [[Dimension 0]] when it collapsed.

When open, it spews a line of lingering fire [[Magic]] at will, letting it be used almost similar to a sword. The fire itself is exceedingly hot and blindingly white, because it is of divine origin. It lingers and burns most of whatever it touches, although it may not combust objects all the time.

It can also act as a regular butane lighter, mainly for Lagrange to fidget with.

## Abilities - Standard

### Spitfire

Lagrange can swipe his hand, spreading a swath of primordial thermite forward, which sticks to the ground and any entity it hits, creating a lingering burn and natural area denial.

### Others?

Per Lagrange's request, Signus keeps him on a strict set of abilities as to control his own pyromania. The list will fluctuate as necessary.

## Abilities - Core Active

Unlike the rest of the core-holders, Lagrange often keeps his core off, as it's sustained plenty of damage and has partially melted. When active, it will emit a loud and uneven grinding sound.

### Thermobaric Burst

Expels a wide and far-reaching cone of flammable gas in a pulse from his wrist. He can choose whether to light it or not. When lit, it will deflagrate and result in a methanol-esque fire, not projecting on the visible light spectrum. While colder than his lighter, it will inherently cause psychological harm along with burns.

### Rebound: Mental Creep

As his core remains active, the deep-set pyromania will begin to emerge. Lagrange despises this feeling, so he'll turn off the core as needed to get his head together.

## Abilities - Overtaken::Lagrange

At a certain point in battles, he will swallow his lighter, igniting his own body internally. This is only done either out of desperation, or when the pyromania takes hold of him too much. The white flames burst from his shell's cracks and the shell loses its ability to break, becoming much more malleable instead. ### Coalesce

Any and all sufficiently weak enough fire, except made by himself, can be absorbed to make himself stronger, and his flames hotter. Do note that at a certain temperature, he will be overpowered and this ability will fail.

### Grapple

He can expel fire from his hand to wrap around the enemy and pull them to him, or move them anywhere else as necessary.
