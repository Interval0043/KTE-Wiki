## Basic Info

Kintoku is an androgynous child in appearance, seemingly around 6 to 9 years old. Their skin is pale, and their eyes have no pupils. their hair is messy and past shoulder length, with a color similar to that of a very cloudy sky, often seen wearing a short cloak of the same color. Although its appearance often changes, due to their "Shapeshifting" abilities, this is the form they take the most often, resembling themselves before death.

## History

As a child, Kintoku lived a life that could only be described as "Unfair", in a public kindergarten. An anti-social child, without parents to stand up for them, was often blamed for things they weren't part of, and no one would care for their pleas if they were being bullied. Not only that, their health was brittle, their skin was fragile and sensitive.

> [!quote]
> ***"My angel would never hurt a soul. They're just playing. Quit being entitled."***

Well, if a whole group of children put the blame on a singular child, then that child will be seen as the culprit for anything. 

Of course, it wouldn't have come to this if the parents paid more attention to their children. Maybe they did pay attention, but were simply blinded by bias, as one day, Kintoku died. All the distracted, ignorant parents heard was the screams of multiple children, and Kintoku's ensanguinated body on the floor. 

Why did Kintoku never fight back?
Maybe they were afraid, maybe they knew they would always be wrong no matter their actions.
But no, they were actually... Hopeful. Maybe they thought that, deep down, if he did his best to do the will of others, he would be accepted. But children who don't know the concept of consequence wouldn't think twice before exploiting such behaviour.

What happened to Kintoku? 
One of the kids found two sharp pebbles, and forced them into Kintoku's nostrils as a 'prank', but Kintoku inhaled hard by accident, bursting multiple blood vessels.
Kintoku bled out before proper medical care could arrive, drowned in his own blood.

He was given a proper funeral. Not that many attended it, but a handful of teachers at the kindergarten, and two or three employees from his orphanage. Some of them were there from obligation, really.
 
Not many years later, upon the events of A Dire Situation, his grave ended up being affected by Dire's final burst of malice before death, bringing Kintoku back as a being merged with malice itself, an incredibly versatile being with all the repressed feelings of the child that now serves as its vessel.

## Abilities

### An unrelenting constant.

One of the few beings able to not only spread, absorb and wield Malice as a weapon, but also to become one with its presence. As long as there's Malice present nearby, Kintoku is effectively immortal. Not only that, Kintoku is in a constant state of growth, being able to use Malice in more and more varied ways- Be it to warp reality to a very small extent, or to create smaller Hands of Malevolence as tendrils. Endless possibilities, really.

### Contempt & Repressed Desire

The desire of change, Kintoku's main ability is that to change the foundation of anything biological and living, and to morph anything not fitting of those categories into them. Allowing Kintoku to shape their body to their desires, and to forcefully morph the body of others by touch. Of course, this wouldnt work on those outside of Kintoku's realm of power, it is still incredibly dangerous.

This only works through direct contact with Kintoku's palms, so they cant just shift into a massive web of silk-thin strings to use it on everyone.
