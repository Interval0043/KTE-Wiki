## History

In the mainlands of the Lukewarm Ocean, twenty thousand years ago stood a simple civilization of decent size, relying on it's agriculture to keep it's denizens fed. A woman named Dâmocles lived alone with her only son, and despite yearning and coveting the throne, looking for ways to reach the top regardless of what it took, she lived a simple day-to-day life.
Since childhood, she saw the world as unjust. Those above took from her, and those below stayed below. She wanted it to change, she wanted the world to become as she perceived perfection, and to stay that way forever.

On the day of her son's eleventh birthday, when she was heading home from the local market, she was ushered to a dark, grassy section of the mainlands, near the shore. She wasn't sure on what exactly compelled her to follow it, nor if she was even following *something*- But it was far too appealing for her to resist. Reaching the destination of this trance, she was met with a man who's face was covered with markings, notably a large "X" in it's center.
He made her a single proposal:

 "I can grant thee the power thou wishes for, I can give exactly what thy soul desires. Yet from there on out, thou shallt not change. Are you to disobey, then from life to past death, thou shallt be bound to your new duties. Thou shallt be enveloped by your position of power, to your core."

Folklore warned her of tricky deities, genies who would grant wishes with drawbacks, yet at the slightest signal of her hesitation, the man spoke once more:

"Have I not chosen thou to rule, Dâmocles? If thou art a nonbeliever, I now become your god. If thou art a believer, thy god reveals itself as I."

She tried to restrain herself, but her mouth quivered in excitement, aching to say "Yes." The wish she held to her very soul since birth, her yearning to shape the world to her likeness and keep it that way, it was too tempting. Suddenly, when her mouth opened, everything went black, and she awoke atop a chair, facing a banquet of royalty within the grand palace of her kingdom. 
She was treated as if she had always been the ruler of the kingdom. She was now at the top and the landscape around her, the way people were now acting all conformed to her idealized rules and behaviours of what a human should be.

... Yet, something was not right. She felt like a guillotine only she could see was hanging by a thread, above her neck. The world was how it should be now, but she was not allowed to change it, and it was not allowed to change her. A life without change, how different is it from death? She neither loses nor gains anything.

That was, until her son finally found her, famished and begging his mother for food. How long has it been since she saw him? To her it has been mere instants, but all this change didn't come instantly, did it? As if to her, time skipped to the moment she aquired her desired world, and her son had no part in it, and as such was left abandoned until said point. His eyes teared up when he finally saw his own mother, who was never emotionally available. Since his birth, he learnt to see her as nothing but a source of sustenance. He gripped her robe with all his remaining, emanciated strength, crying out "Mommy". But she was not allowed to give him food, for that would mean something is taken from her, that would mean she would lose something, that would mean change. Her child was barely even alive, as a matter of fact, he SHOULDN'T still be alive. Yet he lived on, gripping her body, crying and crying for sustenance. A rotting corpse of her son that was simply tormenting her into breaking her contract.

Not only that, by the passing months, she realized flaw after flaw within her kingdom, things she wished she could change but simply could not. Reality was becoming torture, and her heart could not take it any longer. When it was time for her to eat, she gave her son a plate and, while crying, hugged him for the first time in what felt like ages. The guillotine's thread snapped, and both her and her son were put to rest spontaneously, their life simply ceasing to be.

They were buried near the shore, and thousands of years later, when [[Dire Will]]'s death brought one final wave of malice throughout the multiverse, she was reincarnated as the cardinal sin of Sloth, and her son as the cardinal sin of Gluttony.
