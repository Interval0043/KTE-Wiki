> [!abstract]
> Fourier is the assigned [[Deity]] of the [[Hedron]] species, also being the strongest among them. He originally started as a researcher alongside [[Wave]], being the twin of [[Laplace & Hyacinth|Laplace]]. However, as time has gone on, he's become far more of a protector, being granted immense power by his species, turning him into a being of major strength.
> 
> At the end of the day, he's still a researcher at heart. He works currently on the foundations of [[Magic]], carrying a tome with him to work in. He still holds an awe for the world, even if he's grown evermore powerful. While he doesn't show his face often, to those he does, he moves to always make a good impression.

## Personality

Fourier is quiet, thoughtful, and oftentimes nowhere but his residence. He doesn't go out very often, preferring to stay in the comfort of his own home. Still being a researcher, he takes pride in doing things ethically and correctly, often slipping into bouts of getting stuck if something is "right" or not. His engrained sense of righteousness leads to conflict at points, even if he's got good intentions.
