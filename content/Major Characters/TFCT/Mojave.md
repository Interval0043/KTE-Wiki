## History

Mojave's last major appearance was with [[46th Specialized Division|Division 46]] back in [[TFCT]]. Since then, he's been on his own personal journey (soon to be written here), one which has not given him much to work with.

Now, though, he's come back into the light and is working to hunt [[Malice]] on his own time, seeing if good work would be enough to give him some epiphany of purpose.

## Personality

Because of his hybrid nature, his personality is often at odds with itself. On one hand, he's inherently hostile and a general asshole. On the other, to people he's already established bonds to, he's almost obsessive over them and won't let them go. His Slimanoid nature wants to have connections and a support system, but his Mavela nature takes the worst possible routing to do so. It's very tough for the guy to make new friends because of this, giving him the reputation that he has for doing solo operations.

In some genuinely rare cases, though, the Slimanoid can break through and he can finally calm down, giving him much the same personality as Tangent, minus the perfectionism. This can only be done around people who genuinely care for him, although as established before, his qualities make that extraordinarily difficult.

## Appearance

Because of his father's shunning and his lack of parents, he's extrapolated the condemning to come from everyone. He often has himself wrapped in a tough leather, which he has the power to manipulate and move around as he sees fit. There's a small gap for his eyes right at his face, and he's completely mobile in the wear, but otherwise he's entirely covered.

When not covered, for whatever reason, his skin is a really odd consistency. It's a mix between goo and the skin of a Mavela, creating a weird and pale look for the guy. Some sections are fully Slimanoid, that being his left arm and hand, his left thigh, and chest. Some other sections are fully Mavela, being his left calf, most of his right side, and part of his torso. He often wears a simple shirt and thin pants, as he needs to keep himself fairly underdressed for temperature regulation under the leather.

For his color, he takes on the Ivory color of Tangent, but it's considerably paler, coming off more as a pure white than anything. 

## Weaponry - Evoker's Revolution

Mojave's revolver is one with many functions, acting as his main source of damaging enemies. It appears as a more standard revolver, with a longer barrel and custom rear grip. It's held in his left hand, but still has a right-handed cylinder, which makes reloading a little more difficult, as he has to swap hands.

### Ammo

Each cylinder, partial or full, must always contain the same type of ammo. When reloading, he can determine how many bullets to put in, and of what type. If he only wants a few of a type, then he must reload sooner, eating more time.

The bullet types are below. Note the <span style="color:lightgreen">Primed</span> sections. This is when he pulls the hammer back to shoot a bullet at a higher strength or with new effects. Excluding Regular, <span style="color:lightgreen">Primed</span> only lasts for one shot

- Regular: Nothing special, it's your standard .357 round. <span style="color:lightgreen">Primed</span>: Higher velocity and damage. Note: This is the only one that can be used with a speedloader. All others need to be placed in manually. This is also the only one that can remain <span style="color:lightgreen">Primed</span> for multiple bullets.
- Fire: Burns on contact, but lacks bullet penetration. <span style="color:lightgreen">Primed</span>: Acts as a thermite, staying in the enemy and burning much hotter and for much longer.
- Electric: Paralyzes on contact, and will shock nearby enemies. <span style="color:lightgreen">Primed</span>: Instead of shocking and paralyzing, the bullet travels about 5% the speed of light, punching a hole straight through a lot of armor, and with excessive penetration. Retains some Magic quality.
- Metal: Shatters on contact with an enemy, embedding a number of spikes and wounding at the contact point. Useless against armor. <span style="color:lightgreen">Primed</span>: Festers the wound site with a slow-moving necrosis.

### Barrel

The barrel can be extended or retracted, although usually it stays long. Putting the revolver with a snub-nose configuration does have its advantages in ease of motion and concealment, though.

### Soulbreaker's Gambit

> [!quote] All in. No more foolin' around.

There's one last ammo type. The Soulbreaker. This is in a different category of its own, because it's a unique capability of the gun. When a Soulbreaker is loaded in, only one can be loaded and the cylinder is obscured from all parties. Mojave will spin the cylinder 5 times, as hard as he can. If he believes the bullet is ready, he can activate the Soulbreaker's <span style="color:lightgreen">Primed</span> abilities.

The Soulbreaker itself will hit the [[Soul]] of an enemy directly, as opposed to their physical body. It deals true damage, ignoring typical resistances. If <span style="color:lightgreen">Primed</span>, it will have an Execute range. If the enemy is weak enough, it will break their soul outright.

However, provided Mojave doesn't land the shot, two things can happen. If it was a regular shot, the bullet is expended and the cylinder is automatically freed. Another Soulbreaker can be loaded after enough time has passed. If it was a <span style="color:lightgreen">Primed</span> shot, the barrel will shatter, rendering the gun useless for a significant amount of time, until Mojave can replace the barrel.
