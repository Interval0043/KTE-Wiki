## History

Mojave's last major appearance was with [[46th Specialized Division|Division 46]] back in [[TFCT]]. Since then, he's been on his own personal journey (soon to be written here), one which has not given him much to work with.

Now, though, he's come back into the light and is working to hunt [[Malice]] on his own time, seeing if good work would be enough to give him some epiphany of purpose.

## Personality

Because of his hybrid nature, his personality is often at odds with itself. On one hand, he's inherently hostile and a general asshole. On the other, to people he's already established bonds to, he's almost obsessive over them and won't let them go. His Slimanoid nature wants to have connections and a support system, but his Mavela nature takes the worst possible routing to do so. It's very tough for the guy to make new friends because of this, giving him the reputation that he has for doing solo operations.

In some genuinely rare cases, though, the Slimanoid can break through and he can finally calm down, giving him much the same personality as Tangent, minus the perfectionism. This can only be done around people who genuinely care for him, although as established before, his qualities make that extraordinarily difficult.

## Appearance

Because of his father's shunning and his lack of parents, he's extrapolated the condemning to come from everyone. He often has himself wrapped in a tough leather, which he has the power to manipulate and move around as he sees fit. There's a small gap for his eyes right at his face, and he's completely mobile in the wear, but otherwise he's entirely covered.

When not covered, for whatever reason, his skin is a really odd consistency. It's a mix between goo and the skin of a Mavela, creating a weird and pale look for the guy. Some sections are fully Slimanoid, that being his left arm and hand, his left thigh, and chest. Some other sections are fully Mavela, being his left calf, most of his right side, and part of his torso. He often wears a simple shirt and thin pants, as he needs to keep himself fairly underdressed for temperature regulation under the leather.

For his color, he takes on the Ivory color of Tangent, but it's considerably paler, coming off more as a pure white than anything.
