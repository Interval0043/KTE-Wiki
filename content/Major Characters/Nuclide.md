## History

### Formation

Soon after the death of [[Dire Will]], especially in the middle of [[KTE (init)]], more [[Dimensional Fragments]] were spread around. One of which was a Radon fragment, forming and giving us Nuclide. He came to [[Interdimensional Defense & Advanced Research Center|I.D.A.R.C.]] soon after, requiring a specialized suit to function properly around other people. Eventually, he was trusted and knowledgeable enough to earn himself a spot on their staff.

For an unknown reason at the time, his personality formed when he put his suit on, as he seemingly didn't have one beforehand and only acted on pure instinct.

## Personality

Nuclide is often soft-spoken while working, as he's overcompensating for the scare his radioactive nature brings. People don't trust him immediately, and as such, he tries his best to make sure people feel safe around him, going as far as to check his suit's joints in front of them and give them the readouts.

Underneath the soft nature is a curiosity that knows no bounds. Being newer to life than most around him, he doesn't have the same hatred for [[Malice]], only ever seeing it as something to be stared at and studied. He isn't aware of what went on before he was born, and how much damage it causes.

Speaking-wise, he maintains a professional language at nearly all times, and refuses to swear in front of other people, only occasionally muttering it to himself in hopes that nobody hears him. He wishes to be respected, and thinks that people don't based on his nature alone. While partially true, this insecurity tends to be more irrational than not.

## The PPE (Appearance / Soul)

Beginning with the suit, it's more classified as Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), although usually just referred to as "the suit". It starts with the main section, a mostly continuous stretch of specialized and layered fabric to eliminate nearly all radiation exposure to other people. It remains mostly stab, heat, and chemical resistant. Since he has two sets, this main fabric comes in either white (for work use) or bright yellow (for high-visibility purposes).

The goal of the suit is to pressurize Nuclide, and stop him from rapidly decaying and re-forming, which is extremely painful. It keeps his radioactive isotopes stable, which requires complete pressurization and absolutely no gaps except in a single case.

The second goal of the suit is to act as Nuclide's Binding [[Souls|Soul]]. He formed with one, but it quickly fell to radioactive decay due to his extreme nature. The suit takes its place, acting as his personality and mental state.

### Gas Mask

For the suit to function properly, he needs to be able to take in the air around him. This manifests as him "breathing", although it's for a different reason than in [[Human|Humans]]. This is the single case for the gap as brought up earlier, so he can take in air. Due to there being a small bit of airflow, some radioactive gas leaks out through the front of it, but it's a minute amount and is mostly colorless. It decays very quickly and disperses, but still shouldn't be breathed in. His breathing rate is very slow, at roughly once every 30 minutes, as to keep himself stable and to not endanger those around him.

With his two sets, the white suit is paired with a green mask, and the bright yellow suit is paired with an orange mask. It's mounted under the main fabric, clipping into it internally with a tight seal.

### Gloves & Boots

His gloves and boots follow the same coloring as the mask, and serve to allow him to grab onto things, as his Binding Soul is nonexistent.
