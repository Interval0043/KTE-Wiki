## History

### Kingdom

Signus debuted in [[Kingdom]] as one of the many entities who came to [[Dimension 0]] when it newly opened up to other [[Dimension Structure|Dimensions]]. He's considered the first [[Primordial Entity]], not being bound to a specific [[Dimensional Fragments|Fragment]] and wandering the world, hanging out.

### Kingdom SE & Kingdom III

During the events of [[Kingdom SE]] and [[Kingdom III]] he was notably absent, deciding to stay back at his home dimension and work at wood carving from materials he got from [[Human|Humans]]. Within that time, [[Origins]] also bound him to the Ancient Flame Fragment, linking him directly to [[Lagrange]] as of then. Near the end of Kingdom III, he decided to set up in a more rural section of [[Khazingrad]], also to continue his woodcraft.

### TFCT

While taking place in some parts during TFCT, there isn't exactly a whole lot to say about him. He made a reappearance during [[TFCT (Crossing)]], but mostly improved on his woodsmithing. As well, he ended up donning the clothes he wears now.

### KTE

With the events of [[TFCT (Age of Fear)]] out of the way and [[Malice]] back in full swing, Signus has decided to come back into the public eye as an aid to those who need him.

## Personality

Often described as a very laid-back guy by many of his peers, he lets a lot fly and visits people frequently. This is in direct contrast to most other Primordial Entities, who instead prefer to keep to themselves until they are called upon by the people wielding their Fragments. [[Blizzard]] once noted that "nothing really gets to the guy" and he's seen as "one of those guys at a party who you just always want to have". He does what he believes to be the correct decision, almost carrying a sense of moral righteousness and respect for those he's close to.

Now, that's not to say he's lacking in anger. For an entity made of fire, it's quite fitting that he gets his flare-ups. Even with a long fuse, he still manages to get his boots in a twist quite often among those who misuse fire, or are dangers in general. Some have even taking to calling him "police", as endearment of course.

Finally, he enjoys making simple little things using the fire he's made of, especially when it comes to making things for kids to enjoy. He's always found children interesting and special, since he sees them as inherently innocent and can't help but want to keep that intact.

## Appearance

Signus' new wear is very similar to that of a cowboy's, in that he's got a fairly large hat, boots, pants, and gloves. No shirt, but he doesn't need one. All of them are made of some interesting leather-metal compound, giving extraordinary heat resistance as to not burn everything he steps on or touches. Other than that, he keeps a belt on his pants at all times, mainly containing a holster for his lever-action [[Magic]] weapon, as well as an interdimensional radio. He keeps a very simple look, one that's quite recognizable from a distance.

## Abilities

### Standard

Signus, to reiterate, is currently the only Primordial Entity which engages in combat regularly without being so angry as to use a Field Passive. He wanders like a normal person but holds the responsibility of a bound Primordial Entity.

When fully angered OR called down via a fragment, the **Primordial** ability set is tacked onto this one. Normally it isn't, though.

#### Direct Supply

To anyone who needs it, Signus can supply a direct line of Fire to them. This is most useful with Lagrange, but in theory anyone can use it.

#### Bombardment

Signus can summon a barrage of fast-moving fireballs from the sky in an area. He can move the area at which it's hitting at will, and only one can be active at a time.

#### Hell's Bind

Along with [[Scorch]]'s influence, Signus can grip an enemy in hardened brimstone and force them to stay still, along with slowly burning them.

### Primordial

#### Field Passive: Attrition

While Signus is on the battlefield, heat covers everything and afflicts everything except Signus, including the caller. Slowly, enemies and allies alike will burn away, giving the fight a more frantic pace to outlast the enemy.

#### Signus' Distinction

Once in a while, Signus can get to extremely hot flames, his body briefly turning a deep purple. During this time, his attacks, attack speed, reaction time, and movement speed are much greater in scale, but exhaustion creeps in very fast.

#### Reappearances

Anywhere there's fire, provided it isn't on a living entity, Signus can near-instantly appear there. The range is limited to 100 meters at a time.

## Weaponry

### Lever-Action Magic Weapon ("Argus")

> [!quote]
> *Humans already had it good, so let's improve it a lot.*
> \- Signus

Pulled from an unknown dimension, this weapon is modified to shoot customized <span style="color:yellow">magic fire-based</span> shells as opposed to standard buckshot. Medium range when choke is clamped, close range when choke is loose.

#### Modes

There are two firing modes to the weapon: standard fire and extended fire. Standard fire uses a cylindrical magazine on the side of the weapon to load the custom shells in, with a cover on top of it needing to be opened before sliding out the cylinder. Both actions can be done very quickly when done right.

Extended fire, however, uses fire directly from Signus to shoot uninterrupted blasts of much hotter fire, from pellets to a cone-shaped burst flamethrower.

#### Choke

The choke on the front can be moved with a slider right by the trigger.

Loose is meant for up-close engagements when accuracy isn't an issue, letting the magic pellets / fire spread in a wide cone.

Clamped is meant for ranged engagements where a tighter packing allows for higher accuracy. Similar to a slug, but not quite there.

#### Ammo

Signus summons the ammunition, but standard fire uses 30-shell magazines. Extended fire runs off of Signus himself, which functionally has no meaningful limit. Technically it has a limit, which is Signus' supply of fire.

## Attributes

### Fire Drain

- Fire-based attacks will heal instead of hurt by their fire-component.
- Hybrid attacks (kinetic with fire imbued as an example) will still deal Kinetic damage but will heal by the Fire damage.

### Learned Resilience

- Increased resistance to Water, Ice, and Wind-based attacks or components.

### Boosted Presence

- Provides enhanced fire potency when around an allied fire-based attack.

### Expanded Tolerance

- Allows for those without his fragment to call on him, if they have either his or someone else with a fragment's approval.
