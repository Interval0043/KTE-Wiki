## History

This guy's been alive for roughly 84 thousand years, and he's got one hell of a story to tell. Unlike most of the history sections I've done, which are usually told in the third person, these are direct recollections of his that were written down.

### 1: Context

I remember it very fondly, even if I'm aware it was a long time ago.

That little village, of which I fail to remember the name of, was my home for as long as I could have remembered up to that point. Fresh food markets, really cool nature around, it was all amazing.

There was a legend, at some point. It's why we spoke our language and why our houses were the way they were. Legend had it, that every two centuries, something called a [[Human]] would appear and bestow upon us some knowledge. While we really had no evidence, it was our best guess.

### 2: Day Prior

[[Acheron|Arc]] woke me up on a dreary afternoon, complaining about something or other related to a market not having the food he wanted. All I wanted to do was sleep, but alas, that ball of energy was not having it. He damn near dragged me outside, just trying to get me to walk with him to the gym, or something. The minor details aren't important.

On that walk, I was still waking up, and I didn't notice that Arc had left, because some vendor was discussing prophecy and shit. I wasn't buying it, but she said something along the lines of "that whole legend thing's about to strike 2 centuries, we're gonna have a Human", and I didn't pay much attention. Arc, on the other hand, absorbed all of that right up and began taking notes.

I can't help but reminisce on that. He was always so eager, so full of life. *I wonder where he is now...*

### 3: Day Of

It was a solo, calm morning for me. I was harvesting from my backyard garden, when I saw some... disheveled looking creature come waddling up. I would eventually befriend this Human, and nickname him [[Sharp]], which clearly stuck. I ended up finding Arc and introducing the two together, in which case we began to absorb the new interesting speech. English, as he described it, was this unique thing none of us had ever heard before, except for one vendor calling their fruits "mangos".

When we brought Sharp there, he simply said: "I don't know what kind of mangoes those would be. But those aren't mangoes. They're bright fuckin' green. Ours are orange." Of course, I abridged the dialogue, but he was quite the more vulgar man when needed. Seemingly casually so. I ended up learning that was a common thing where he came from, but I decided to not pick that habit up. A few other Slimanoids did end up doing that, though, and it became really odd to talk to them.

Just as with Arc, *I don't know where he is now...*
