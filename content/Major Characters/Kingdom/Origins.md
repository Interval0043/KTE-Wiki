> [!quote] **"What... are you?"**

> [!abstract]
> Origins is a [[Primordial Entity]] who does not usually take up a humanoid form. He presides over his own realm, and usually IS the realm, a place where he can take people and talk to them if he so chooses. Usually, this is done with the people who have [[Dimensional Fragments]]. He also presides over the rest of the Primordials who govern fragments, while not governing one himself.

## History

Origins hasn't done much over the roughly 30,000 years that [[Dimension 0]] has been gone, except occasionally create Primordial Entities for specific [[Dimensional Fragments]] that are discovered to him. However, as things begin to stir in [[Eternal Infection]], he's finally decided to get out of his little hole and do something about the [[Malice]].

## Personality

Origins is often seen as being a little bit playful, but also aware of his strength possibilities. He wants to exist as nicely as possible, and he takes a fascination in everyone he works with. This too bleeds into his appearance and language, as he'll quite often mix formal talk, off-putting slang, and simple language. For him, it works. He rarely thinks about if it works for other people, though.

In having been the manager of a realm, though, he does not know very much. His fascination usually exists alongside him commonly misunderstanding things about other entities, yet never in any malicious way. Alongside his unknowing nature exists the second issue of him not knowing where his strength lies exactly. He knows of his upbringing, and he knows some of what he can do, but most of himself is a mystery to him. One thing that's constant, however, is his watchfulness. He's always looking at something, always watching someone.

He is classified as a [[God]], currently only managing other [[Primordial Entity|Primordial Entities]]. He has a strong sense of politeness and mischief, but also a very wishful thinking structure. How he thinks is different than most, as he doesn't have a whole lot going on inside his head. There's a chance he can feel negative emotion, although that's usually few and far between, as he prefers to let the people he manages do the fighting. Every so often, though, he will step in and show a lot of care at seemingly random opportunities.

## Appearance

He will commonly appear as chunks of broken buildings, fluids moving about, plasma, plant life, or just about anything else. He doesn't maintain a consistent form, especially when in his realm. However, if he so needs to exit his realm, he will take up the appearance of a humanoid, at precisely six feet tall. His body will be primarily ruined brick and vines, with his hands being a mix of that and other vague connecting substances. His "face" is an odd assortment of gas, fluid, and plasma, making eyes and a mouth that float there. Binding it all is some simple telekinetic magic, nothing too special.

Clothes-wise, if he's in his human form, he looks surprisingly formal. He takes inspiration from three-piece suits, where other colors of concrete and steel form a "blazer" and "tie". He keeps everything simple, often finding a want to swap outfits on the fly to mimic who he's near, if he takes a special interest in them.

## Residence

His most common area of residence is his own realm, where he seemingly both is and isn't visible. It's unclear what exactly his realm is, all we know is that it exists.

Inside of his realm, there's a bunch of stuff floating around, and people can seemingly stand anywhere they wish, even where there isn't supposed to be anything. It's a very surreal space.

## Support Abilities

Origins' main deal is providing new capabilities to people he trusts to do good, with these abilities.

### Blessing of Creation

Gives the target a much easier way of manipulating or creating matter to be used (see [[Axioms#Axiom of Transfer]]).

### Direct Power Transfer

Usually done to a [[Slimanoid]], he can provide them significant increases to their strength for battles. Most often used with [[Blizzard]] or the [[Slimanoid Main Lineage]] for their legacy and previous good will.

### Warp

An emergency teleportation to be used on a target. Origins has the ability to return to his realm at any time, and by holding the person and teleporting back, he can Warp them. Usually, though, this will incur some manner of nausea or disorientation, passing through typical resistances, as the teleportation is messy.

### Restructure

He can deploy and expand fragments of his corporeal body onto the battlefield to give advantages to who he wants, although this may give the enemy an advantage instead. He does not have the greatest tactical sense.

The goal is to turn the battlefield from what is normally a flatter area into one with tighter corners and more decisions to be made from both sides. Origins, again, does not actually know this.

### Partner Provision

He can call on a fellow Primordial Entity to assist the person he chooses, although the one chosen usually follows the abilities of the person he's helping. Depending on who he calls on, they may listen and assist, or deny the request and leave. He'll try a few others to see, but there's a good chance the ability fails.

## Offensive Abilities

> [!quote] **"Me? Fight? I... don't know how. I don't know what to do."**

He doesn't have any, yet. Due to his naivety in a lot of things, he's never learned what fighting looks like, nor how to do any on his own.

Should he actually take up combat, most of his fighting style is about experimentation. He does random bullshit until it works, or he finds something he likes.
