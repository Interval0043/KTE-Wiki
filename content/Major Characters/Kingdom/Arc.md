In recent time, though, he's lost an eye through a long-drawn conflict, opting to be highly distrusting and bitter instead. > He's described as a brawler, primarily focusing on close-range offense and quick movements. He almost always works in tandem with Blizzard, who usually backs him up and enhances his abilities, although their dynamic is very fluid.

## Personality

Arc's personality fluctuates often between bitter and friendly, depending on two factors. If he's awake and around new people, he tends to be very vocal and confrontational, not trusting anyone at first sight. However, if he's exceedingly tired, or around [[Blizzard]], he'll be much friendlier, opting to keep himself quiet and let Blizzard do the talking.

In competitions and combat, he does everything he can as to not lose, opting to talk shit while fighting to rile the opponent up, often preying on stigma that he doesn't believe in to get an advantage. He plays dirty, as he learned many times before that fighting honorably is only a detriment.

## Appearance

He's an red-orange homogeneous [[Slimanoid]], much the same color as [[Alter]], with a loose structure and five fingers on each hand. He stands at 6 ft 4 in, preferring to keep himself short as to move around better. He'll also fluctuate between semi-loose and very loose, letting his figure squish and stretch around as he pleases.

Clothing-wise, he's seen frequently in tank tops and very loose shorts, keeping it just to that as to let himself have freedom of motion. Sometimes he's seen wearing a thin zip-up jacket, but that's either for memory reasons or for specific occasions when he needs it.

## Battle Style

Oftentimes, he's in a Pair Battle with Blizzard. The two of them frequently swap roles of defense and offense, even though Arc doesn't have that great of defensive capability. That lack of defense is made up for by Blizzard's high natural defensive abilities, as well as a competent offensive game.

Arc tends to go for rushes, preferring to overwhelm and play dirty rather than have a truly "proper" fight. Every one of his hits is meant to be powerful by nature, focusing on high kinetic output, with added [[Magic]] capability through Blizzard.
