## History

### Pre-Lab

From 1952 to 1963, he was a normal child in the BSSR (under the USSR), formerly named Alyaksey Harun. At the age of 11, his family needed money, thus selling their child to a lab in rural USSR, specifically near the top of Siberia. 

Not much else is known, as his family is most likely prisoner to the USSR and Rorik’s memory was partially wiped, giving him Retrograde Amnesia prior to his lab experience.

### Lab

From 1963 to 1971, he was subjected to experimentation by the aforementioned lab. Eventually this resulted in a full brain transplant into a rearranged wolf body, as well as injection with certain medication and steroids.

Now a humanoid wolf, very strong at that, they looked to turn him into the perfect soldier to eventually begin a hot war with the USA. As such, they also forced him to learn English, to translate between their language and the USA’s. They had named him "успешная вещь", translating to "successful thing". As he was their only success on this specific project, they nicknamed him "Rorik", which stuck with him. It was going fine, as they had begun the
memory erasure phase, until an oxygen leak caused an explosion, letting out a few subjects. This cascaded into a riot, letting Rorik escape.

In his escape, he grabbed two weapons: a knife, and a Makarov Pistol (PM), along with ample ammo. He also picked up a brown coat and hat of the time, embroidered with Soviet symbolism, as it was thought to originally belong to a service member of the Red Army.

### Post-Lab

From 1971 to 1973, he lived in the top of Siberia, foraging for food and existing as he is. In 1973, a rift shot open and ripped him from his dimension, pulling him into the outskirts of [[Khazingrad]].
