## Overview

Originally used to refer to the Malevocatus species, it is now used as an umbrella term for sub-species originating from the [[Malice]]. This includes the [[Seven Cardinal Sins]].
## Categories

While not all Maledictions fall under these categories, these are still useful to identify common phenomenons and beings that are directly related to the Malice.

### Malevocatus
Corpses being brought back from the dead in the form of rotting, seemingly mindless, bruise-purple and red zombie-like creatures, often infesting more populated areas and places such as graveyards. Despite their apparent unintelligent behaviour, they often target those who were in any way related to the past life of the reanimated corpse, as if haunting their old loved ones, acquaintances and enemies.
Malevocatus vary largely when it comes to power and danger, as the species, the individual and the size of the Malice Source that reanimated the corpse. The longer a Malevocatus lives, the more powerful and more sentient it becomes. Due to being composed of mostly Malice, all types of Malevocatus have high regenerating capabilities.
The Malevocatus have their own sub-species, nicknamed Putridum and Fusum respectively.
#### Malevocatus Putridum
Malevocatus Putridum are corpses that, while physically inferior to their regular counterpart, are constantly covered by a liquid-like, red substance they secrete, the texture of which resembles that of vomit or spoiled milk. This liquid-like substance, when in contact with a living being, be it through an open wound or any other orifices, slowly ceases their body's vital functions such as their hearbeat, or for non-organic creatures, the flow of electricity. During this, the target will experience sharp pain, similar to that of having vibrating metal nails inside your head and muscles, tearing through the flesh and shocking the nervous system, which is eventually followed by violent spasms throughout their entire being. Grotesque distortions on the face and body are often observed before the victim finally dies, turning into yet another one of the Malevocatus.
#### Malevocatus Fusum
Living tissue of any kind blasted off from Malevocatus or even some other Maledictions are by technicality still alive, and often flop around like a fish out of water. They slowly clump together and fuse into an uncoordinated clot of different kinds of flesh, that eventually synchronizes and gains proper movement. They eventually gain a mouth, and attach themselves like a barnacle at the epicenter of a Malice Source. This awakens the instinct of other Maledictions to feed it, causing it to digest the "Food" into Malice in a process much faster than the Malice's natural spread, which it then shares with the Malice Source for it to grow alongside it even more rapidly.
