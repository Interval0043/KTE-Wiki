Kruula are bulky, golem-like demons with a hard, almost rocky exoskeleton They instinctively gravitate themselves towards chaos and destruction, sensing fear that brews a strong desire for souls. They hold the power of BLIGHT, used as a catalyst for their reign of power over souls.

A majority of the Kruula that exist were formed inside of the Eidolon Cavern, where lingering remnants of souls from Dire’s aftermath (and other unfortunate souls from other dimensions) were reincarnated. Many onlookers of Unstable Catalyst lacked the understanding that many of these poor creatures lived in a cruel, never ending battle for both survival and spiritual freedom; As Kruula weren’t at all to meant to relish in anything else but mindless slaughter.

## Lifespan / Maturity

While a young Kruula is rare, most Kruula in early development are often described in nature as ravenous and mischievous, having stronger (and more uncontrollable) desire for souls than adult Kruula. This psychological trait is essentially a more extreme form of an eating disorder, and can (but rarely) persist even through adulthood. Young Kruula don’t have a glow, and instead, their parts that do glow are completely transparent at birth, and their “glow” will eventually brighten over time.

Kruula enter a regressed state of development when they have an absence of food for a varied duration of time, which follows the glow on their teeth, eyes and markings dimming/graying out. They are physically incapable of dying of age unless they starve, which spans anywhere between 1-2 months (varies more they conserve their energy or not). 

Their rocky bodies also crystallize over thousands of years to become reflective, making them easier to spot; Kruula generally grow to be more impatient and frustrated because of this.

Typically, elder Kruula can last much longer without food: The diminishment of their glow means they use less of their own energy, which gives them about an extra month to spare.

* Newborn (0-1 yrs)
* Infant (1-5 yrs)
* Toddler (5-10 yrs)
* Childhood (10-100yrs)
* Adulthood (100-1000+ yrs)

## Dependencies

Kruula rely on harvesting and maintaining the life-force of other deceased entities to sustain themselves, their process usually involving [[Magic#Dissonant Energy (BLIGHT)|BLIGHT]]: An anticoagulant to prolong the Kruula’ parasitic hold on a soul. Long periods of time (Usually 1-2 months) without any sustenance will either result in death, or a shift into the elder (regressed) stage.

## Appearance/Variation

The color of a Kruula’s bodily features is purely cosmetic (E.g: Eyes, markings, claws), though the color usually stems from what the Kruula’s soul was before they underwent the transformation process. A transformed Kruula may carry some physical characteristics from their previous incarnation, for example: Ears, tails, or even special features such as venom or other specialized evolutionary abilities.

Their eyes have a semi-transparent, solid color. While they appear to have no eyes. Their “hollow” looking sockets have thousands of tiny, microscopic eyes. They can also close and open their eyes, though Kruula are inexpressive in body language, and are forced into a cold death stare.

Kruula have a rocky exoskeleton with small, crack-like fractures that separate their individual plates. The texture of the exoskeleton is rigid, however: these plates can be sanded out to be smoother similarly to their claws/nails. Kruula also have long, sharp quills in their hair, though they are hard to make out. Like beetles, a Kruula’s wings are hidden by their exoskeleton. 

Only female’s are assigned with wings at birth, with male Kruula being too large and bulky for wings. Female Kruula are typically 4-6ft tall, while males are 8-12ft in height, giving a clear indication on what sex is which.

## Psychology

The difference between us and Kruula is that they’re instinctively driven by the fear of others, survival, and death, loud noises, majority are born with a sadistic nature. A Kruula’s soul is given the same “feel-good” effect by absorbing soul energy, taking lives, and inflicting pain in others; This doesn’t mean that they’re evil, as Kruula are capable of feeling regret, and other forms of emotions many other species share.

However, Kruula are naturally desensitized to death; Meaning Kruula simply just don’t react to others or their own kind as drastically as other species do. Unlike other species, loud noises aren’t associated with danger, and instead are associated with food. The same applies with a Kruula’s ability to sense fear.

Young Kruula have a psychological effect called “Psychoactive Life-Apathy” (PLA), which is something that ties together with common food disorders (e.g: BED). This disorder grows more severe as it develops through adulthood, and can be characterized by the Kruula feeling the need to even cannibalize their own species. Elder Kruula usually follow the same things that come with old age.

## Soul

A Kruula’s soul is in the shape of a vertically stretched octahedron, the color assigned during/after the transformation process. Like before, the color is completely cosmetic. 

These souls are large, larger than the souls of most magical beings in a body-soul ratio. This is because their souls act as a bank that stores and feeds off of their essence using BLIGHT magic, which is a destructive, anomalous form of magic that feeds off of and prevents the coagulation of other souls. 

They do not follow the same rules other souls do: they do not base themselves off of “traits” meaning emotions do not have the capability of altering/warping the world around them. Their souls also have a slight resistance to malice.

## Biology

A Kruula’s biology is a little more a streamlined, even if they are a freak of nature. They are a hyrbid of a collective of these specific creatures: Beetle, Mosquitoe, Porcupine, but mostly Golem. A Kruula’s biology is built around surviving the extremes of Unstable Catalyst, involving extreme gravitational fields and smoldering temperatures. Kruula also naturally resist malice, and any other phenomena of Dire’s influence.

Their build, both female and male: center around blending in with their surroundings or ambushing, but both usually apply in a grouped setting. A Kruula’s only weakness (universally) is diagonal mobility due to their internally gelatinous body.

### Hair

All Kruula have barbed, hidden spines layered around their head. These spines are venomous, hidden under the Kruula’s hair. Whenever the barbs of a Kruula is exposed, it’s a very uncomfortable experience; A part of maintaining Kruula hygiene is not allowing for the hair to be unkempt or short. 

If these barbs anchor, they detach and inject BLIGHT. A Kruula has control over these barbs, being able to lower and raise them if necessary.

### Claws

Under the plates of a Kruula’s fingers lies a magical, gelatinous substance similar in magical composition than that of the rest of their innards. This structure hardens to form different variations of claws for the Kruula (both the hands and feet). For example, these claws can become hook-like to latch onto prey, or grow longer to become blade-like.

### Feet and hands 

Kruula have feet/hands that share similarities to a gecko, with small, tightly-knit plating that appear to be “folds”. Kruula have tiny, microscopic hairs between them that they can retract, as to not stick to every surface they’re on. The way their feet and hairs are structured leaves them disadvantaged in diagonal mobility due to shortcomings in maintaining balance and grip; Their astounding mass clashes too much with even advanced evolutionary traits. Males are most affected by this disadvantage.

### Mouth

A Kruula’s jaw structure is vaguely similar to humans, but which kind of teeth a Kruula has varies. However, a huge difference is that a Kruula’s jaw can lock, requiring far more resistance to force it back open.

A Kruula’s tongue isn’t static either, sharing similar molding capabilities like the claws and feet. The proboscis is sensitive, hidden underneath the tongue, which serves as protection when feeding. The proboscis is responsible for direct feeding and injection, while the tongue also doubles to help swallow the soul down.

### Eyes

Kruula have thousands of eyes, though appear to be hollow sockets. Like insects, these thousands of eyes give them an extraordinary, panoramic view that allows them to track fast movements. Kruula have access to thermal vision, too. 

A Kruula does not need to blink often, but if they do, their reliable, albeit limited structure within the ridges of their eyes shields them.
