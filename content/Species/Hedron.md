## Species Overview

The Hedrons are a fairly rare species, with an estimated 10,000 being an upper bound. Due to their unique formation criteria, with the added requirement of needing a [[Dimensional Fragments|Dimensional Fragment]] in most cases, they don't come about very often.

They're primarily composed of a shell, a binding [[Souls|Soul]], and an inner substance that's usually a gas of some kind. Often, they also have a [[Dimensional Fragments|Fragment]], but that isn't necessarily required (see: [[Variate]]). 

## History

### Early

The Hedrons began in [[Kingdom SE]] as a direct descendant of [[Dimension 0]]'s falling apart nearing the end of [[Kingdom]]. Formally, they were formed from some of the Dimensional Fragments, specifically one each. It's important to note the first three Hedrons, in order, are [[Kepler]], [[Poinsot]], and [[Lagrange]]. They got their start in [[Khazingrad]], and that location continues to be a major hotspot for the species.

In [[Kingdom III]], more were discovered to have formed in other [[Dimension Structure|Dimensions]], migrating to Khazingrad or settling where they formed at.

This earlier time isn't too interesting for the Hedrons, as not much was going on. There was still the lingering threat from Kingdom to worry about, but other than that it was a very peaceful time for them to get their bearings in a new world.

### Close Past

Beginning in [[TFCT (Prequel)]], the sister species to the Hedrons showed up, known as the [[Manifold|Manifolds]]. A major point is that while they're both considered [[Kingdom-Formed]], they're distinct in their properties and the species don't cross [[Deity|Deities]]. It's worth it to note that the first Manifold is [[Interval]], as later on he'll work with the species directly.

In [[TFCT (Crossing)]], the two species mix and it works out due to their common ancestry, as well as needing to work together to continue fighting the threat established in TFCT (Prequel).

Throughout the rest of [[TFCT]], the Hedrons continue being discovered and their numbers grow. Eventually, growing closer to [[TFCT (Age of Fear)]], most of them go into hiding and wait all of it out.

### Eternal Infection

Now, after hiding, the species has banded together their most important members and have made it their mission to continue rebuilding places and to foster lively areas in a time where many are uncertain. Others choose to instead take direct protection, working against [[Malice]] and trying to reclaim what was once theirs or others'.

## The Shell

The shell of a Hedron is their most recognizable feature, as it's what's seen on the outside and makes up a good portion of their appearance.

> [!example] Defaults
> - The shell is made from an interconnected set of metal triangles, often made of some composite metal with random composition that doesn't really make a difference. It's semi-breakable, with the ability to hold electricity and localize power when broken.
> - Its default color is a light gray, as many metal alloys tend to be; with a smooth and cool surface.
> - Normally, they do not have internal supports, only existing on the outside of the body.

As with anything, there's a lot of knobs to turn when it comes to the shell. Quite a lot are listed below.

### Composition

The shell itself can have differing composition. An example is [[Eiro]]'s shell primarily being made of a more Magnesium-centric alloy, allowing for him to spark it and blind enemies quickly. Depending on the metal, it may take on different properties, like being malleable instead of brittle, breaking like tempered glass instead of shattering, or being especially conductive, and that's left up to the creator to decide.

### Color

The simplest of all. Just the color they'll be. Allows for a lot of expression with them, where there otherwise wouldn't be. Lumped into this is also how the color presents itself, with a few options like matte, glossy, rough, translucent, etc.

### Structure

While the default structure is free-floating interconnected triangles, there's more to it than just that. First is adding supports, like with [[Laplace & Hyacinth|Laplace]]. His shell is much more resistant to collapsing inward, and the supports allow it to break in less places when fractured. There's also a possibility to stack layers on top of eachother, potentially having different ones do different things.

It can even go as far as to change the shapes that form it. I know triangle & square can work well, and so can hexagons, so there's a lot of cool geometry to be had with it.

### Other Properties

The thing about shells, is that you can get extremely creative with them and add in other properties. The ones listed above are just the base 3, but any other silly and stupid things you want to come up with, go for it! There's a lot out there to explore when it comes to metal and things, like work-hardening, better torque handling, or other structural / material engineering concepts.

## The Binding Soul

The Binding Soul is the thing that ties everything together. While it remains one of the least customizable things about Hedrons, it still stands to have a few knobs to turn. It sits as a selectively permeable substance between the Shell and Inner Substance, and keeping their body together.

Typically, it contains the Dimensional Fragment, but it can also contain other links, or nothing at all.

## The Inner Substance

The inner substance of a Hedron is what allows them to keep their shape, along with the binding soul. Notably, it can come as just about anything, but it most often presents as a gas. Below are a few things that can be tuned.

> [!example] Defaults
> - The Inner Substance is a nickel-based homogeneous gas, which serves as basic stability for the Hedron.
> - Other than the basics, it serves no specific purpose.

### Phase

It can be in one of a few phases of matter, which are listed below.

- Liquid
- Gas
- Plasma
- Supercritical Fluid

 > [!warning]
 > Any others do need to be passed by the author, and Solid is **strictly disallowed**.

### Composition

The composition of the inner substance gives it the strength it has, how flexible the body is, and how well the soul is defended. Much like the shell, it's a lot of material engineering. Some things to think about are listed below.

- Amount of distinct elements / molecules
	- Homogeneity (if there's multiple)
- Viscosity
- Conductivity (in relation to Components)
- Malleability

### Color

This needs a color too. It'll usually present as a sort of underglow behind the shell's color, or nothing if it isn't bright. Depends on how the Shell is done.

Hedrons will naturally inherit everything within this category by default, and no abilities will be listed UNLESS there are meaningful deviations, in which case an Inheritance callout will be used.

### Abilities

#### Breakshot

This amounts to deliberately fracturing one's Shell to localize power in the hand. Upon striking an opponent, the localized power violently releases, significantly enhancing the impact but also injuring the user somewhat. Depending on the scale at which the user can heal their shell, this is either a desperate play or a staple of one's kit.

#### Discharge

Due to the Shell being able to conduct electricity, it too can be held within the shell and released through a conductive surface. Depending on the Hedron, it can be easier or harder to release said electricity, and also it changes what conditions a Forceful Discharge can take place, where the electricity is released in a burst around the Hedron rather than at an enemy.

#### Integration

A Hedron (or [[Manifold]]) can take up a new substance, species, or whatever, and Integrate with it. What this entails is letting it into its own body for studying reasons, in a sort of sandbox environment. If well-contained and well-managed, the Hedron can gleam new abilities or forms, along with new understanding. If done incorrectly, the Hedron can be injured or taken over entirely.

While this is risky, it can be very effective.

### Attributes

#### Semiconductive

- Increased Electric resistance.
- Increased conductivity.

#### Shatter

- The Shell will naturally spray fragments at whichever entity broke the shell except oneself.
- Said fragments will cause moderate lacerations.

#### Adaptive

- Ability to wield multiple forms of [[Magic]] at once.
- Increased analysis of situations.
- Increased Integration speed and odds.

### Instincts

There is only one instinct of note, and that is the Call. Every single Hedron, unless otherwise stated, will drop everything and assist a fellow Hedron if they're close enough or visible to eachother.
