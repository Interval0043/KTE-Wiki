## Formation

The Primordial Entities form in one of three ways:
1. A dimension collapses in on itself, giving it a chance to create an entity based from the dimension.
2. One is formed by another Primordial Entity for a given purpose. So far, this is done with some of the Primordial Entities bound to the [[Dimensional Fragments]].
3. An already existing entity is promoted to a Primordial Entity and given a position. This is not reversible.
4. A non-bearing Primordial Entity is formed by other means. They can be promoted. Promotion is not reversible.

> [!example]
> For examples of the above list, see these:
> 1. [[Origins]]
> 2. [[Arkochet]]
> 3. (none yet)
> 4. [[Signus]]

## Who They Are

While there's no consistent look to them, they all usually take up a body representing what they come from or are bound to. This is so they are easily identifiable when they are summoned, or when they are referenced by other people.

When it comes to design, however, pretty much anything is fair game for how they look (clothes-wise) or how they act. Even as entities of great strength, all of them have a need to be individual and will maintain separate personalities as much as possible.

> [!example]
> [[Signus]] is a good example of the above.
> 
> He's known to be a humanoid made of only fire and nothing else. What kind of fire is currently unknown. Taking on the personality of almost a cowboy, he presents himself as a chill guy, being one of the only ones to actively talk to the people he's bound to.
> 
> He wears boots, pants, gloves, and a big hat all made of insulation of some kind, as to prevent him from accidentally burning whatever he walks or touches.

## Shared Properties

First, each and every Primordial Entity must have something called a "Field Passive". These affect the whole of the battle in specific ways, but do not necessitate they apply to themselves. Some level the field to bring enemies closer in power, others do weird things, and some are just plain useless and are there for flair.

Second, Primordial Entities have to respond to something to summon them. Whether it be a [[Dimensional Fragments|Dimensional Fragment]], a call from someone line [[Origins]], or something else they care about.
