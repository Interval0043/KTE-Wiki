## Basics

Primordial Entities are those formed within the harshest of conditions, sometimes a dimension collapsing, other times through a powerful entity's own creation process. However one is made, it's almost a guarantee that they either are or will be very strong.

Most of the well-known Primordial Entities work under [[Origins]] as managers of the [[Dimensional Fragments]], however they don't have to. They may just as easily fall under [[God]] or [[Deity]] status as well, depending on what they do.

Notably, most Primordial Entities are also very one-note, taking the appearance of whatever they were formed from, or whatever they manage, as to remain distinct at a glance. Just as easily can this not be the case, however, as their volatile formation requirements lend the species to outliers.

## Formation

Primordial Entities are formed through a few known ways.

1. A dimension collapses in on itself. See [[Origins]].
2. Another entity, Primordial or not, creates a Primordial Entity. See [[Cosmos]].
3. A Primordial Entity is formed through an entirely different process that is cataclysmic or extreme. See [[Theia Astral]].

However, the above list is not exhaustive. The nature of the species is that no two creatures are the same, and with how few of them there are, there's bound to be new methods around to make new ones.

## Fragment Manager Subtype

Oftentimes, though, method 2 above is used to create entities of which to manage the Dimensional Fragments and the people who are created from them. Usually, said entities are created by Origins, but sometimes can be promoted into the position, like in the case of [[Signus]]. Fragment Managers will almost always work under the supervision of Origins as well, although they remain autonomy in who they are and how they manage. This can sometimes bring friction between Primordial Entities over who is doing their job in the better manner.

If one is a Fragment Manager, they will each have a Field Passive and their own set of specialized Primordial Abilities that relate to whatever [[Dimensional Fragments|Fragment]] they're assigned to.
