## Overview

The Malice works as the raw, primordial material that creates and sustains it's sub species, [[Malediction]]s. It wouldn't be quite right to call it matter however, as while it behaves similarly, it originates from [[Dire Will]]'s home dimension, [[Kahurea]]. Thus it can not and should not be defined as "Matter", regardless of how close it may be to it. There isn't a set rule as to how the Malice can manifest or look like, but as a baseline, red and black seem to be the main but not the only coloring of it, and a lot of the time it looks like a mass of rotting flesh corroding and converting anything nearby. And although not common, sometimes they may take the form or color of something that should not and does not exist within reality, causing psychological distress and strain to anyone gazing at it or even attempting to remember what it looks like. In these cases, it is not recommended to attempt and make sense of what it even is.
Research into the Malice is incredibly difficult, as not only is studying it incredibly dangerous due to it's ease of breaching, there isn't really anything to reference or look out for as it is a completely alien *thing*.

## Behaviour

This will go over the general behaviour of The Malice. There may be exceptions to what is described here, but those should be, for the most part, restricted to the sub-species of the Malice.
### Spread
The Malice naturally spreads and devours what it can, getting larger and larger as long as it is not stopped. It's spread is for the most part restrained to what is physical, but when needed, it seems capable of spending more of it's energy, as if it was a living organism, to spread through what is not physical, too -- Such as space itself, and in the worst case scenarios, when there is nothing else to consume, through the axis of Time. Dimensions where said scenario takes place are doomed to become an Eternal Malediction.
Being "Consumed" by the Malice is a painful process that transmutes what is into something that shouldn't be, causing physical and psychological suffering.
### Creation
The Malice is capable of granting chunks of it's mass, or corpses and bodies it has consumed, consciousness. Every being within the Malice has a mental link of some kind, allowing them to act as individuals but also as a hivemind that follows the pecking order of the Hierarchy.
### Hierarchy
The Hierarchy of the Malice, which allows it to be coordinated without much if any in-fighting, is a biologically hard-wired concept to each and every being within the Malice, with Dire Will sitting at the very top, and all of the [[Seven Cardinal Sins]] sharing the very next spot.
### Cleansing
The Malice is naturally extinguished by raw [[Magic]]. Similarly to how Anti-Matter mutually eradicates Matter, Magic mutually ceases to be alongside the Malice, although without any of the violent energy release that comes with Matter Anti-Matter annihilation-- that was just an analogy.

## Important Events

### Dire Will's Death
Upon the death of Dire Will, seven shards of it's Soul or Being were spread throughout the multiverse, each allowing for the embodiment of a sin by a Malediction, creating the Seven Cardinal Sins. Not only that, Dire utilized it's final remnants of power to generate a massive wave of Malice across the entirety of EI, causing severe changes to the very structure of the cosmology, including the dissolving of the natural barriers between dimensions.
