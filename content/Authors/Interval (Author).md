Hey, I'm Interval or Polytope, and I'm one of the many co-owners of KTE.

Now, I got started when [[Sneiard]] invited me to this little imagination game in the 5th grade called [[Kingdom]]. Since then, it's remained in my head, and a few years back, I found [[Psychotropicz, Crazer|Crazer]] too. Soon enough, we took on [[Spookie]] as our third co-owner.

This has turned into one gigantic soup of people, characters, species, and a story. I couldn't be happier with where we've come, and I'd love to see where we go in the future.

By the way, my stuff listed below is free to use as inspiration, or to make your own OCs based on! I really stress creativity, so give it a read and maybe you'll find something you like!

The list down here is not the most comprehensive, but I'm trying. Also, the headers are just links, not necessarily things I've made. Look for the bulleted list for things I've made. Clarifications are provided as needed.
