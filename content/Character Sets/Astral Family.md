## History/Basics

The Astrals, referred to as the Astral Family, is a lineage sticking to one central theme: All of the Astrals have a cosmic influence that is embedded in their genetic code to awaken their dormant, powerful forms of magic.

They do not view themselves as gods, merely mercenaries, reflecting upon the countless centuries they spent trying to better their living conditions inside of [[Unstable Catalyst]]. Despite their tremendous feats of strength and power, they were the main punching bags of the outlandish world they resided in. 

A bitter queen that ruled over Unstable Catalyst placed huge bounties (for the sole purpose of enslavement) over their heads, along with the rest of her bloodline due to her; physically and psychologically exhausting the family after countless attempts of staying under the radar. No matter where they went, they still lived like prisoners due every faction aggressively pursuing to enslave them.

This era was a devastating experience for the family, some describing the experience as a near-eternity of suffering dating all the way back to when to the second-youngest generation reached early adulthood, facing the same issues and ridicule their relatives had experienced time and time again. 

Miraculously, they managed to escape from that world and into another dimension through a powerful spell [[Theia Astral|Theia]] casted after [[Dire Will|Dire]] had massacred a large portion of the Unstable Catalyst, where they eventually found a nearby, undiscovered universe. What lied beyond that world uncovered a purpose; something Theia hadn’t truly envisioned, but that purpose was negotiable. They liked sticking their heads where they shouldn’t be.

## Traits & Nuances

Going over the traits of the astral family is quite interesting, especially because of how their genetics aren’t entirely set on specific, “luck of the draw” traits. 

Instead, when a family member is born, the magic they’re all given is predetermined at birth, tied to the information of a random celestial body (E.g: Black holes, Stars, planets, asteroids). For example, if a new soul is assigned a certain star at birth, their soul, vessel, sex, or other physical characteristics have the possibility to be affected.

Initially, astral family members thought that once they were assigned to a certain group of magic, they weren’t learn anything else outside of what the magic they were assigned (due to limited information they had on themselves). However, they realized that they could learn the same types of magic the rest of the family had been born with.

Reishon had a rather impressive share of traits that strengthen the endurance and physical strength of each member of the bloodline, ensuring that no soul or vessel is developed weak or dysfunctional.

Theia’s genetics are also another important component, as they play a critical role in the rapid growth in their cognitive development and their magical capacity. She is also the reason why they are born with the specialized souls.

Lastly, as each member passes down these traits from generation to generation, the traits that originate from both Theia and Reishon are always present, meaning that new generations will always carry the genetic material that influences the creation of the soul.
