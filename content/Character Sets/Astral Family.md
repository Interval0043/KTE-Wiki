## History/Basics

The Astrals, referred to as the Astral Family, is a lineage sticking to one central theme: All of the Astrals have a cosmic influence that is embedded in their genetic code to awaken their dormant, powerful forms of magic.

They do not view themselves as gods, merely mercenaries, reflecting upon the countless centuries they spent trying to better their living conditions inside of [[Unstable Catalyst]]. Despite their tremendous feats of strength and power, they were the main punching bags of the outlandish world they resided in. 

Tyrants that ruled over many militias in Unstable Catalyst placing huge bounties over their heads; physically and psychologically exhausting the family after countless attempts of staying under the radar. No matter where they went, they still lived like prisoners due every faction aggressively pursuing to enslave them.

This era was long and difficult time for the family to finally truimph, describing the experience as a near-eternity of suffering dating all the way back to when to the second-youngest generation reached early adulthood, facing the same issues and ridicule their relatives had experienced time and time again. 

Miraculously, they managed to escape from that world and into another dimension through a powerful spell [[Theia Astral|Theia]] casted after [[Dire Will|Dire]] had massacred a large portion of the Unstable Catalyst, where they eventually found a nearby, undiscovered universe. What lied beyond that world uncovered a purpose; something Theia hadn’t truly envisioned, but that purpose was negotiable. They liked sticking their heads where they shouldn’t be.

## Traits & Nuances

When a family member is born, the magic they’re all given is predetermined at birth, tied to the information of a random celestial body (E.g: Black holes, Stars, planets, asteroids). For example, if a new soul is assigned a certain star at birth, their soul, vessel, sex, or other physical characteristics have the possibility to be affected.

Initially, astral family members thought that once they were assigned to a certain group of magic, they weren’t learn anything else outside of what the magic they were assigned (due to limited information they had on themselves). However, they realized that they could learn the same types of magic the rest of the family had been born with.

Reishon had a rather impressive share of traits that strengthen the endurance and physical strength of each member of the bloodline, ensuring that no soul or vessel is developed weak or dysfunctional.

Theia’s genetics are also another important component, as they play a critical role in the rapid growth in their cognitive development and their magical capacity. She is also the reason why they are born with the specialized souls.

Lastly, as each member passes down these traits from generation to generation, the traits that originate from both Theia and Reishon are always present, meaning that new generations will always carry the genetic material that influences the creation of the soul.

### Wish Upon A Star

> [!note]
>  This is a rare trait, could be considered the WAR of the Astral Family.

The ability to call “Stars” (which will eventually dissipate over time) in any given point, which will gradually become more difficult to summon as more are made into existence. These “Stars” are physics and reality-defying objects capable of allowing the user to manipulate time, reality, and bring abstract concepts or ideas to life.

There are 1-100 stars the user can create, following a percentage value that holds a general idea of how much influence they hold over the world around them (e.g 1% wouldn’t be able to do much, as opposed to 100%). These stars are both summoned through sheer willpower, while also shaving off magic in the process.

Magical capacity is the key factor to keeping the ability afloat, as the stars can be re-stabilized if the user so wishes. However, prolonging the existence of these stars (especially if multiple are present) can be quite exhausting.

These stars cannot be destroyed through conventional means, meaning they can only be destroyed through magical disturbances that tamper with object’s reality-altering properties.

The user, as stated before, can bring nearly anything into existence. The size and scale varying by the % value. Usually, anything above 5-10% can create subtle, or even noticeable differences.

### Duality

Due to their magic, and very essence being deeply tied to celestial bodies; The state of "Day" and "Night", as arbitrary as it may be in certain situations, can affect their performance in battle.
When a member of the astral family is born, they're assigned a "Polarity", being nocturnal or diurnal.

During night time, Nocturnal members will gain a non-negligible enhancement to their magical capabilities, limits, and output during the night, while a diminishment of said abilities of the same scale will occur during the day.
The same is true, albeit in the opposite timeframe, for Diurnal members.

### Cosmic webbing

The user of this trait is able to create threads of the magic they were assigned with at birth, with the use of the webbing varying on each user. Generally, this ability is either used for area coverage, mobility, or as a direct source of damage or to ensnare opponents.

For example: A user assigned a star with this trait has their strings extremely hot and plasma-like.

If the user is more experienced with this trait, eggsacks form in the web, which hatch out their own form of spiders to attack the opponent if caught inside.

### Otherworldly Traction

Just like spiders, who can cling to walls and or otherwise impossible surfaces without issue; The user with this trait has their reflexes modified to where they are nigh-impossible to be knocked down, minimizing friction through a process where gravity is shifted constantly. 

For a example: A user may be pitted against winds that would bring them off of the ground, or otherwise do anything that may force an adjustment. When the trait is in effect , it appear as if the wind never had an effect on them in the first place, allowing them to be just as mobile.

Another example of the ability is the user being able to place a foot on a wall, and then being able to walk up it with no issue, and the list goes on.

### Comet

When born with this trait, an astral family member can transform themselves into a state that coats them in thick layers of their own magic that hide their form, leaving a trail of magic behind to closely resemble that of a shooting star.

This grants them speed proportionate to the total amount of magic they possess; but generally, this ability grants them a far greater speed than they could even perceive. This means that the user is capable of traveling over long distances with little effort, also granting them flight to travel over long distances. However, at a high speed, it difficult for the user to change the trajectory of where they’re going.

## Structure/Norms

This family is a mess, no doubt. However, it is kept in place by Theia Astral, who serves as the co-director of Leigon Of Nazareth, and is the eldest of the entire bloodline. A majority of what they do is in favor of her, but for good reason.

This family’s survival is deeply rooted in their self-preservation, problem solving skills, and ability to negotiate. A very good reason why these exist is because of their time in Unstable Catalyst, a world with poverty-stricken, dystopian societies. This means as a collective, they are very independent if need be, with an unwritten rule being that interactions are seen as mostly transactional. However, this doesn’t mean they cannot do favors or other simple tasks out of companionship, and even if some are so, they were simply just raised that way. This behavior is more frequent in older generations.

This family loves trading.

This slang is used to describe anything that is inherently difficult or incomprehensible to understand, such as Malice. For example something “Singu” would be the destruction of the anti-void caused by aftermath of the fight between [[Dire Will]] and [[Counter Will]]

## $\text{V-I-R}\;20^{7\cdot 15}$ / The Net / $\Omega$ (Omega)

Inside of this impossibly dark cave holds a colossal, underground network of celestial webs that goes by three distinct nicknames (though mainly referred to as “Omega”), serving as one of many distinct fragments behind its origin.

Its main purpose is to manifest and store all forms of conventional and unconventional energy (magic, etc.) within the omniverse. This does not mean it holds energy equivalent of one omniverse, it simply means it holds energy so diverse that there is nothing virtually nothing it can’t power or give energy to. Omega also has demonstrated a worth of its own not just to the Leigon Of Nazareth, but the Astral Family as a whole.

Due to this mathematical equation (Ohm’s Law) being manifested as a tangible object or entity, it leads to ongoing instability that only those with enough intricacy and flexibility can stabilize, and as a result, Omega requires the near and absolute perfect conditions to for it to serve its purpose.

### Appearance

#### Inside / Middle

Omega’s physical appearance resembles a huge, pentagonal pyramid suspended in the middle of the air, weaved from many layers of black, plasma-like webs flowing with ever-changing, glowing reds, pinks, and purples that occasionally scatter light like an aurora. Other less structured, miscellaneous shapes like sheets weaved by the Mind Weavers act as supports for the pyramid in virtually all directions. The flexible structure of these webs were designed to be easy to navigate, even for bipedal entities.

There are huge, around 50x50 feet tunnels of networked silk that spirals upwards to form a spring-like structure, obscuring any potential view of what it may look like inside just from looking at it. From up above lies many sheets which many Mind Weavers perch themselves upon, acting as gatekeepers to the sacred pyramid’s entrances.

#### Outside / Entrance

The cave itself is impossibly dark, where the only thing that can really be made out as well is the lights that emit from the webbing. There are stone stairways that lead down from the massive trapdoor from Theia’s quarters, which serves as the entrance for this place. 

With the ceiling home to gargantuan stalactites, the floor is quite the opposite with the entrance to the cave being riddled with all sorts of fenced, tiled pathing; The pathing branching off from each other that encircle Omega in ring-like shape. 

Various sorts of celestial berries hang from dense, overhanging vines that give off the effect of distant stars, doubling as a cosmic garden.  In this garden lies architecture consisting of overpasses that allow for members to cross the deep, narrow ravines that hold the vast majority of the berries seen. Another addition to this cave is the carefully constructed pools built around the ravines that hold lava, pouring down endlessly from hundreds of feet above which are used by the family to lounge/bathe inside of.

### Procedures

To ensure the web’s stability, it is necessary for the family to adhere caution and follow these crucial principles before properly utilizing Omega. Omega, despite being “just silk” is extremely unstable when there is no field to maintain it, and must be handled with utmost care and patience.

Firstly, one member will review analytics on the stabilization field the whole way through its use, and report to Theia if there are any anomalies within the set of given data. The second step is a monitored process where Theia, or other highly-knowledged members on Omega to interact with Omega briefly before allowing other members to operate.

### Mind Weaver Tarantula 

They are social, intelligent arachnids with psychokinetic abilities. They display unbreakable loyalty to the mega structure, deemed as "Guardians Of Omega," They hold charge over minor repairs, fending off intruders, and preventing other potential shortcomings only Theia’s expertise can handle. With rigorous experimenting done, [[Legion Of Nazareth]] perfectly shaped these entities into a symbiotic relationship between themselves and Omega, as they share mutated fragments of Theia's genetic coding. 

In appearance, they're mainly jet-black with the coloration of hairs resembling that of the beauty within Omega, releasing glowing, nebula-like lights that correspond with their patterns. What they most closely represent is the jeweled tarantulas on earth, except they can grow to be much bigger, and can also have differing eye color which also emits light.

These creatures (at least in comparison to the Astral Family members) lifespans are quite short, living only 80-100 years. Usually once Mind Weavers hit adulthood, they start laying egg sacks asexually. However, Elder Mind Weavers (which grow no bigger than small building) are given heavy, armored plating made from Omega, and are sent out with other Astral Family members on dangerous expeditions, giving them more of a purpose than essentially being glorified cattle. 

These odd arachnids also weave a clear, secondary silk used for the second layer of an egg sac, which is often harvested to allow for a member to tap within the deepest corners of their memories, manipulating the shape of the silk to create various forms of celestial equipment.

### $\epsilon$ (Epsilon)

While apart of Omega itself, this pentagonal pyramid was given its name because of one of its many purposes it holds inside, namely the ability to shrink down users into impossibly small sizes. The appearance of the inside of Epsilon is described as an open, compressed spherical space, with a relatively even floor and ceiling. 

From virtually all sides, it holds all sorts of chests and racks full of artifacts and magical equipment. 

#### Use #1

Upon the will of a member, they can create a bright light that shrinks them down to an impossible size, creating an expansive pocket of quantum space that serves as training grounds for the Astral Family to experiment with.

What allows these pockets of space to work and not become destroyed themselves is that Omega drastically decreases the power of the user by storing it inside of Omega. However, inside of that space it appears as if their psyche has not been affected. The only way for an Astral Family member to notice the pocket in space is seeing a large glow in the area the space the member created it in, then promptly focusing magic to create a flash of that light to shrink themselves down.

#### Use #2

Mind Weaver silk from harvested egg sacks can be used by the astral family to create various forms of magical weapons, armors, artifacts, and specialized equipment that aligns with their celestial trait. The creation of these usually takes 1-2 weeks, with larger items like full sets of armor taking a month. Usually, items are made by hanging the webs in a cocoon of silk from the ceiling, then when they glow brightly enough, the cocoon comes apart easily to reveal the product.

### “Alpha” / “α”

> [!quote] *It feasts upon all… A rapacious, insatiable object desired only by those who wish to strip the world and themselves away.*

A legendary gauntlet that appears to be a nearly formless entity, held on a pedestal in middle of Epsilon. The gauntlet is held in a vat of Theia’s own blood; The only thing remotely strong enough to supress the anomalous, psychological allure it gives off. This entity is made from pure anti-magic, a being manifested from the deepest, most empty vacuums of space that stretch on endlessly for hundreds of millions of light years.

#### Use

To put it simply, this entity grants the ability for the wielder to control anti-magic. Think of anti-magic like anti-matter to matter, it cancels eachother out and reacts extremely violently. The reactions of different forms of magic with anti-magic may vary, but exclusively this form of anti-magic is aimed for a nigh-absolute cleansing.

A victim may suspect the loss of their magic, but it’s subtle enough to slip by untrained eyes. In short, they cannot feel their power being drained away. It may appear as if an entity could use power to their full potential, but they just couldn’t for some reason. This gauntlet doesn’t just strip away power, it creates an illusion that they still have what is now gone forever.

This effect also applies to the wielder, as wearing the gauntlet for too long can leave permanent dysfunction in anything that is magic-related.

The true power of Alpha lies in those fleeting moments when the wielder wants to do what will be done.
