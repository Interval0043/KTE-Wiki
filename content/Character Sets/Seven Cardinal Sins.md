The Malice created seven specific beings, designated as the Seven Cardinal Sins, in the form of incredibly advanced [[Malediction|Maledictions]], that act as countermeasures to powerful beings who can easily mow down a large crowd of weaker enemies. The Seven Cardinal sins are imbued with the sin they represent, and a small shard of the primogenitor of the Malice's corpse. They have a much deeper connection to [[Dire Will]], and due to this, have incredible potential. Alongside the obvious benefits that come with this, every Cardinal Sin is capable of absorbing [[Malice]] to recover their energy and become stronger, along with being able to spread it themselves.

## Inheritance: Abilities

The Cardinal Sins all share two special abilities in common. However, each of them also take on an ability relating to the sin they represent. This section will go over all of these abilities.

### Universal / Shared

#### Insatiable and Unrelenting

Upon death, none of the Cardinal Sins will simply cease to be. As long as two conditions are met, they will be reborn within an indefinite amount of time, be it hours, days, months, years or minutes, in a different form or body. The vacuum left by the death of the previous Cardinal Sin will soon be filled by it's reincarnation.
The conditions that must be met are:

1. Malice must still be present. If all Malice is to be eradicated from the multiverse, the Cardinal Sins will cease to exist.

2. The very sin they represent must still exist, within the same time-space as the Malice. As an example, if the concept of "Greed" itself is not present in the same dimension as any sources of Malice, be it by the lack of any intelligent life forms or any other reason, the sin of greed may not be able to be reborn, until a life form who has indulged in the sin of greed in their lifetime arrives. As such, separating the Malice from all other space-times, would also cease the reincarnation cycle of the sins.

#### Transitory Apotheosis

A fluctuating ability that is, by technicality, out of the user's own control. This ability occurs most often during intense emotional moments, be it for the user, their target, or even during difficult battles.

When the user strikes an opponent physically, reinforcing their attack with magic, there's a chance for a distortion to occur in which the Malice transmutes the energy into Impossibility Magic, not only causing the attack's properties to change, but also, for a split second, allowing the Cardinal Sin limited access to Dire Will's memories, skills, reflexes and muscle memory.
This significantly boosts a Sin's learning curve for magic or anything related to Malice and Impossibility whenever it happens.

### Exclusive / Individual

#### Unyielding Hunger (Gluttony)

The want for more, the lack of a capacity to be satisfied. Currently wielded by [[Kintoku]], allows them to devour whole and absorb the powers, memories, abilities and energy of, albeit in a very diluted version, anything it wants.
And the matter absorbed does not go to waste. Kintoku stores it inside their own body, compressed to infinitesimally small "pods" that are used to replace their wounds, without the energy use of creating matter from thin air.

#### Unsustainable, Inconsolable (Wrath)

The irrational anger, the explicit desire to harm others. Currently wielded by [[Euphen]], allows for every bit of anger, ill intent, every act of aggression and every thought of doing so, be it towards Euphen or from Euphen towards others, to be stored as "Energy", causing Euphen's flames to flare higher. This can then be released as flame-like energy, blood red in color, that cares not for allies or foes. That cares not for defenses or anything of the sorts, it's only purpose and intent is to destroy.
This ability tears through defenses as if it weren't there. Harms even Euphen himself or other malice entities as if they were foes. Does not stop at attacking the body and corrodes the soul and mind as well. A psychological, physical and metaphysical attack all at once.
