Hello, and welcome to the KTE wiki. There's a whole lot going on, but I'm sure you'll figure it out soon enough. To begin, the sidebear is your best friend. Clicking on folders will pull up everything that's in them.

Also, you may see a graph off to the right. Clicking on it will expand it, and allow you to see what pages directly connect to the one you're on. Optionally, you may also be able to view the global graph, although I'm not sure how helpful this may be.

Made with hot glue and dreams, hosted from [this GitHub Repository](https://github.com/interval0043/KTE-Wiki), as well as using Obsidian and Quartz v5.
