The Legion of Nazareth is a large organization separated into seven departments. The LON’s main goal, quite differently from the original Legion of Havoc, is to watch over the universe of [[KTE]], acting as a sort of “Watchtower” to look out for any threats or issues, thus the name, “Nazareth”.  
  
The funding of LON comes from organisations affiliated to LON, such as their shipping companies, technological companies, etc… Which transfers most profits back to LON.

All employees of LON will wear an armband, wherever as long as it’s clearly visible, with exceptions such as during missions or during off  hours, that distinguishes them to their department. For example, the Research Department employees wear armbands that are an opaque yellow in color, with a golden symbol depicting a microscope.  
  
Employees of LON are given varying degrees of a similar technology, nicknamed “Nazareth PDA”, or “NDA” for short. These PDAs are portable, high-tech devices oval in shape made from a smooth white plastic, with a touchscreen on the flat side. Made by [[Glaci-Tek]], the NDAs have a multitude of functions such as the ability to hover over the air, create holograms of UI or video calls, and many others.  
  
Order of command (Per department.)  
\- Ruling Dep. takes priority.  
  
Director > Co-Director > Commander > Officer > Agent > Employee  
  
Other positions such as medical or gastronomic ones, will likely fall under Agent or Employee.

## Research Department

The Research Department is a department that works directly in unison with the Development Department. The job of the Research Department is to study and discover anything possible about new materials, beings, objects, artifacts and magic.  
  
For the most part, workers of the Research Department work in laboratories with containment cells and equipment fit to whatever work subjects are present at the time, provided by the Welfare department whenever requested.  
  
As stated in the prior guide, the Research Department employees wear an armband depicting a golden microscope in a plain, opaque yellow background.

## Development Department

The Development Department’s main function is to work with the information provided by the Research Department, using said info to create and develop technologies, weapons, medicine, antidotes, and countermeasures to anything. Their purpose is to give LON as large of an array of possibilities as possible upon the encounter of any and all issues.  
  
Often working in more risky and unstable projects than the Research Department, The Development Department labs have as a base rule, inner walls coated in Nullium. In case of accidents, the Development Department is quite catastrophic, due to often working with new, destructive weaponry. Thus, Nullium walls are necessary to keep the destruction to a minimum by not letting it breach outside of the laboratory, which would often cause LON Bases to collapse.  
  
The Development Department wears opaque blue armbands depicting a golden Niel’s atom model, having aquamarine hems.

## Containment Department

The Containment Department is a department that works directly alongside the Research Department, similarly to the Dev. Department. Its job is to safely store and contain unknown, problematic or important subjects of any kind, mostly found via the Scouting department.  
  
In the Containment Department’s work spaces, there are contained and secured objects, materials, souls or entities that may be used for research unless deemed too dangerous to be worked with. The levels of security for each subject varies between what is deemed necessary.  
  
The Containment Department employees wear a pitch black armband with red hems, and a golden depiction of a padlock.

## Scouting Department

Despite what one might think, the Scouting Department has a larger role than it may first appear. Although not as heavily armed as the Power Department, the Scouting Department carries decent weaponry and works on, well, scouting the areas protected by the Legion of Nazareth. Along with that, they’re tasked with the control of exporting or importing of any goods, along with often being issued to collect intel on important areas, and are ordered to retrieve any found artifacts or beings of note to the Containment and Research departments.  
  
Though not exactly a “Work Space”, the Scouting Department commonly has exclusive break rooms and cafeterias due to their heavier workload on most Legion of Nazareth locations.  
  
It is also to be noted that, the Scouting Department works with the Power Department when dealing with cross-omniversal travel, such as a more recent example, enforcing the limits applied to foreigners from RCT from bringing objects and/or artifacts from our reality to theirs.  
  
Members of the Scouting Department wear a brownish green armband, with dark green hems and a golden depiction, which is the symbol of an open eye.  
  
The scouting department is supplied with steampunk styled weaponry, notably a spear controlled by the user’s magic, with thrusters on the back of the spearhead that can allow it to fly, change directions or simply enhance strikes.

## Welfare Department

One of, if not the most essential department. The welfare department is responsible for keeping locations clean with its janitors, treating the wounded in its myriad of medications and information provided by the Development and Research departments with its doctors, and much more, ranging from simple assistants that bring and prepare the proper tools to other agents and employees, up to the ones who write and relay summaries of mission, projects, or test results.  
  
The Welfare Department’s employees work in such varied locations, that the “Workspace” of theirs is required to vary as well. Ranging from storage rooms, receptions, up to surgery rooms. They, alongside the Scouting Department, also get access to the exclusive break rooms and cafeteria, although this only goes for the Welfare Department members with more responsibilities, such as the doctors.  
  
Welfare Department employees wear many different color schemes of armbands depending on their professions, ranging from dark green ones with deep blue hems for janitors, to plain white ones with vibrant red hems for doctors. Though, all of them have a golden depiction of an upside-down A.

## Power Department

The “Armed Forces” of the LON. The Power Department is home to not only the most skilled and powerful individuals of the Legion, but they also get access to only the highest tier of weapons, and most recent technologies. The Power Department has the simplest jobs out of any department, though arguably the most dangerous as well. They are the guards, the soldiers, the bodyguards, etc… Of the Legion. Basically, LON’s Military.  
  
The Power Department doesn’t have a workspace, but they do have access to the higher end editions of a technology nicknamed Porter Shape. Porter Shapes are small, electronic objects which store the information, shape and mass of everything inside a set space. Upon activation, the Porter Shape pictures the stored information back into reality, effectively instantly transporting objects and structures by the press of a button. The Power Department gets access to Porter Shapes which carry entire military dorms, so they can rest during missions without leaving the site. Porter Shapes usually carry smaller objects, only the more advanced ones can carry objects to this extent.  
  
Power Department employees wear armbands that are pitch black in color, with vibrant red hems. They have a golden depiction of two arrows crossing each other, forming an X.

## Ruling Department

Effectively the main governing force of the LON other than [[Draconic Will|Draconic]] himself. The Ruling Department is responsible for the creating and changing guidelines and rules of the Legion. Their responsibility also stretches over to deciding on punishments and choosing or changing the course of action within missions or projects. They also directly relay orders from Draconic.  
  
The Ruling Department’s workspace is usually a tidy, black tinted meeting room with printers, electronics and many shelves with physical prints of documents and folders. It has noise-cancelling walls as to avoid non-authorized personnel from overhearing confidential information.  
  
Employees of the Ruling Department wear a gray armband with dark yellow hems, having a golden depiction of a lightbulb.
