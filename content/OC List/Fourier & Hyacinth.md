### Hyacinth

#### Former Occupation

Hyacinth came from a job in Hell requiring a security clearance, for the worst of the worst to be in there. As part of the torture, the residents being tortured aren't allowed to hear of the outside world at all, therefore all outside material was banned.

Working in said job, it was both stressful and boring. Stressful in that there were a lot of people to manage, and boring in that there wasn't anything else to do or anywhere else to go but torture people. His complacency in his line of work and an improperly stored torture device lead to his head being impaled, severing the left occipital nerve and exiting through the left cheek.

Because of this injury, Hyacinth changed to being how he is now: timid. This also caused a chunk to be broken off of the left horn near the base, causing the horn to be somewhat unstable and require a bit more attention, otherwise it would snap inward and break off.

#### Leaving Hell

Some time after the workplace incident, [[Scorch]] bestowed upon Hyacinth a book about, of all things, nature. Hyacinth found the fungi and poisons sections to be particularly interesting, and set out. After a few more smuggled books, he made the journey and exited hell.

In the forest, there were enough poisons to satisfy Hyacinth and begin his collection, with him learning that he has especially good resistance to toxins. After enough poisons were collected, he realized that plants were supposedly sprouting from his tail and horns. After careful examination, it seemed as though the seeds for which the plants originated were there from the beginning, only being able to bloom because his potential was being realized.

#### Crossing

While Hyacinth was continuing to exist in the forest for some time, [[Interdimensional Defense & Advanced Research Center|I.D.A.R.C.]] came in to mine uranium. [[Variate]] found Hyacinth, calling upon Fourier to take a look at the demon. Fourier saw the damaged horn and took Hyacinth in for a repair.

After repairing, though, Fourier began to feel a bond form, that of a fictive kinship. He ended up taking him in as a younger brother, giving Hyacinth a place to live and Fourier someone to talk to so he isn't going crazy being alone in his home.

## Fourier's Details

Fourier is one of the more important [[Hedron|Hedrons]], serving as one of the species' main protectors.

### Personality

Easily described as stern, caring, and vengeful. Being who he is, he cares for all he can. Put him in a situation with someone in danger, and he's the first to jump at it. However, in that same vein, he also teaches his own very well. He's fine with nonsense, but disregard for others is not at all something he supports.

His vengeful streak only occurs once someone attacks one of his own species, specifically a prominent member. He doesn't get all pissed over a nobody, but if someone like [[Kepler|Kepler]] got seriously injured for a not very good reason, then he's after whoever hurt him. However, justified injuries (i.e. Kepler began the fight) don't get him all riled up.

Finally, there's one more bit. To those he considers adjacent family and not descendants, his care shows a lot more than the teaching. And for [[Hallis]], a lot of his personality is wiped in favor of formality, considering he's second at the top of the Hedrons.

### Clothing

Fourier wears a very thick hood-less coat, with no gloves, and a set of strong boots. This arises from his main residency being in a very cold area.

### Relations

Fourier is widely considered the "dad" of the species. Not as in a blood father, but as in a father-figure, caring deeply for all of his own and teaching them what they need.

Him and Hallis, though, are the one exception. He stands second in command to the species' [[Deity]], performing ground operations when Hallis is too busy being the species' god. The two share a sort of genuine father-son relationship.

Fourier took Hyacinth in, feeling a different instinct. Less of a fatherly instinct, but more of a brotherly one. Fourier acts as Hyacinth's handler only in writing, frequently being more of an older sibling.

## Hyacinth's Details

### Personality

Hyacinth is considered an introvert due to his quietness and antisocial nature.

### Appearance

He wears a green t-shirt with long sleeves underneath, as well as cargo pants and combat boots. Sometimes, he'll have a star clip in his hair.

As a part of his demonic nature, he has a chain tail with a mini-scythe blade on the end, with horns.

### Relation

Fourier is the only person he knows, and the only one he will be around for an extended period of time. Because of his personality, he finds it difficult to make new connections, seeing Fourier as his older brother.
