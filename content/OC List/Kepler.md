## History

### Kingdom SE

Kepler is known as the first [[Hedron]], formed all the way back in [[Kingdom SE]]. Second is [[Poinsot]], third is [[Lagrange]], and the three are referred to as ”The Triad”. Despite them being the oldest, they’re not the strongest, nor are they that big of protectors. However, one key detail is that they are considered tight-knit siblings, with Poinsot as the sister and Lagrange as the brother.

### Kingdom III

Not much in specific happened with Kepler during [[Kingdom III]] aside from what happened in the main plot. After reuniting with the main cast from [[Kingdom]] and doing what they needed to, Poinsot and Lagrange were lost near the end of Kingdom III. He's willing to kill for his siblings, as he made moves to find them, leading him into the brunt of TFCT.

### TFCT (Crossing)

In said mission of finding his siblings, he met [[Interval]], a person whom he respects for what he did to aid him. Although, he's been concerned as of late, citing that "he's changed, and he won't talk to me". He still owns Interval's own MP443 Grach from long ago, but keeps it in a display case and refuses to use it.

During this time, Poinsot and Lagrange were recovered and found safe. From there, Kepler began finding more of his species around and making bonds.

### Duplication?

At some point, [[Eiro]] showed up. Described as Kepler but from a different timeline, he decided to stick around for a while. Kepler didn't exactly know what to do about it, so he just went along with it.

### Anecdotes

#### Inception

I woke up one day, surrounded by water. I don't remember anything, and I don't recall having ever been alive before. I began to feel light, floating to the surface. Seeing a dock, I grabbed ahold, pulling myself up onto it, although I remember it being a struggle. There was another, who looked similar to me, so I felt the need to help them up too. Me and someone else, sitting on a dock in an unfamiliar place. I would soon know myself to be Kepler and the other person to be my dear sister, Poinsot.

> [!note]
> The place mentioned in the anecdote is [[Khazingrad]].

#### Brother

I would soon learn of there being a third. The residents of this place thought we were some gang, all "wearing" the same clothes as identifiers. We were mistaken as begin accomplices while being completely uninvolved and oblivious. Eventually, we found him temporarily held up in a police cell. He looked like us, but his shell was burnt a bit and he didn't seem too happy to see us. Through the coming months, there would be a bit of a petty rivalry between him and I, trying to wrestle between doing the good thing and fulfilling one's own desires. I began to understand him; he was held in the grip of his own head, craving to burn things as a way to escape what he got himself into. This man would soon be my brother, Lagrange. And yes, he's gotten much better as of late.

#### Infection

It's an odd feeling, not knowing where you are. I felt trapped in my own mind, and I sometimes still do. I could see through my own eyes, but I couldn't do anything, my body moving on its own, slumped and limping. I can see my siblings and friends moving me along, fighting for me while I wander and do nothing.

After a near eternal-feeling wait, I feel clear after a mental blackout. I come to my senses, seeing something large dead and my sister hugging me. I look around, seeing my friends battered but grateful. I don't understand what happened, until I hear the voice in my head speak up: "You're welcome. I got you through this. Now understand. We're bound by life and death, needing to keep eachother alive. I've done my part, now you owe me."

Cordyc's reminiscing as I write this. It continues to remind me how he "saved" me, although I didn't buy it then, and I certainly don't buy it now. Can't say it's been bad, it's been a very good arrangement. My own Celestial Parasite, stuck with me.

## Personality

Kepler's often described as being one of the more childish Hedrons around, but it comes with being one of the most genuine people out there. Genuine doesn't mean honesty, but he definitely isn't afraid of being who he is. Oftentimes he's seen around Poinsot, but when the two are separated, he doesn't feel as confident and prefers to stay out of the spotlight whenever possible.

## Clothing

Awhile ago, he got a heated, padded, blue zip-up coat. He frequently keeps the heating filaments on, and the hood up. He also usually wears jeans and loafers, so only his face and hands are visible. This is just to keep him warm, as his body naturally runs cold. When his Celestial [[Souls|Soul]] activates, it tends that the hood of his coat will fly backward.

## Relationships

The first two of note are his brother and sister, Poinsot and Lagrange. He's been with them since the very beginning, and he'll do anything for them.

With many others, he maintains a solid friendship, being as supportive as he can. However, he can't be everywhere at once, and finds maintaining all of these to be stressful, opting to eventually prune ones he doesn't need or haven't interacted with in a long time. Of course it's on good terms, or at least he tries to stay in their good graces.

In terms of love, Kepler's a little lost. Hedrons are notable for it being extremely hard to feel any attractions other than platonic, with most being outright impossible to maintain something romantic AND genuine. Kepler, though, seems to be coming through and seeking something deeper, although he doesn't know where to begin.

#### Feint

When hitting an enemy, Kepler can choose to apply this ability, which will make the impacting surface (what he's hitting the enemy with), glow blue. If the enemy attempts to block this attack, a significantly boosted attack will be thrown out automatically from an unpredictable direction. The second attack may be blocked, but due to its speed, it requires a higher reaction time. The follow-up, if blocked, self-damages due to haste. If it's successful, it's more damage and a stun.

A natural cooldown will be applied. No rigorous cooldown will be specified yet, but do understand that not every attack will have Feint applied.

#### Channel

He can channel the energy in his core into an attack, letting the impacting surface glow a bright red. Whether or not he lands the strike, Doubt will be applied, considering his core is temporarily deactivated. If the strike lands, it has significantly more power, but Doubt lasts for longer as a result.

Doubt is one of Kepler's immutable traits normally, but in battle is considered a debuff. He won't go for physical attacks unless the enemy gets close, in which he gains a special Hasty Counter. His perception is up, but his movements might be sloppy and he's a little fearful.

#### Hasty Counter

> [!warning] Requires Doubt.

Upon an enemy getting close enough with an attack, he can let off a Counter. Not that it does much damage, but it's his attempt at trying to defend himself.

#### Auto: Determined Continuation

Provided he gets injured enough, his core will naturally Overclock, pushing his doubts away and keeping him in the fight. Empowers his attacks as he drops in stamina, but will slowly lead to desperation.

#### Celestial Awakening

Awakens Cordyc. Here's the hightlights:

- Celestial Soul is activated
- Attributes and abilities are swapped Kepler $\to$ Cordyc
- <span style="color:lightblue">DISCHARGE</span>: Releases all stored electricity in a burst around Kepler.
- Kepler's shell breaks and suspends, floating in the air as bits, pieces, and chunks. His primary form is replaced with his inner gas.
- Kepler's consciousness takes a backseat, being replaced by Cordyc.

#### Auto: Celestial Backlog

Cordyc will naturally grow restless over the course of combat. In the event it doesn't see some action to satiate its hunger, it will begin to lash out at Kepler, causing him a searing sensation all over, narrowing Kepler's options significantly. However, this may also be a sign that Cordyc is primed for combat if Kepler is struggling.

#### Energy Burst

When Celestial Backlog is active, he can fire off the backed-up energy as a high-power near-hitscan projectile. Due to the urgency of firing it, his accuracy is penalized somewhat. If he concentrates, he can hit the shot, but the pain makes it difficult.

When released, it will let off a loud boom of sound, rattling Kepler. Rattle is one of his special statuses, which is simple: he's in that sort-of shock state for a little bit, breaking his focus.

#### Cordyc's Insistence

Provided Kepler goes to a critical state, Cordyc will pick up the slack and Externalize, causing it to revert to its original parasite form and shield Kepler. Kepler is rendered completely immobile in a curled position, being protected. They will no longer attack, rather begin finding an escape route.

Provided an escape route is not found, Cordyc will decide to force control and fight viciously as a last-ditch effort. If there is one, they'll take it immediately and leave combat. Despite defense being up, they are still definitely killable.

This may trigger early if Cordyc is starving, halting Kepler's combat.

### Cordyc's Abilities

#### Imperium Fractionis

Fires rough pieces of Kepler's shell, at a fairly fast rate. Intended to stick into an enemy. Can be retrieved; when retrieved, the pieces act like barbs and tear out.

#### Lacrima Caelestis

Rips a tear in the sky, ceiling, or anywhere, and focuses celestial energy as a beam. Cordyc can choose which celestial body it focuses from, which can change the effects. Currently known bodies are listed below.

- Stars: Fire Pillar
- Neptune: Ice Lance
- Other Gas Giants: Wind & Debris Tunnel

#### Gravitas Tractatori

> [!warning] Math incoming.

There's a formula we use in Newtonian Mechanics called the Law of Universal Gravitation. It states that the force of gravity is equal to a constant, multiplied by the mass of the body drawing AND the body being drawn, all divided by the distance of the pulling body squared.

Or, in short, $F_g = \dfrac{G \cdot m_1 \cdot m_2}{r^2}$. Now, Cordyc can define a point in space to have really high mass and drag things toward it. Or, it can redefine an object to have a different mass. The former makes a static gravitational attraction, the latter makes two things approach each other.

#### Occultans Fumum

Using the gas from celestial bodies, it can fill the field with a temporary thick smog.
Can be seen through with thermal equipment, but Cordyc's thermal signature blends in with the gas. Not completely, the fragments don't blend and will look very different. A color difference is also visible; the smog is primarily a brown-black while Cordyc is much more colorful.

#### Metallicum Compage

Returns Cordyc to Kepler. All changes mentioned in Celestial Awakening are reversed. Depending on the actions it took, Kepler may feel exhausted upon return.
