## History

Soon after [[Dire Will]]'s death, multiple [[Hedron|Hedrons]] were dragged together and formed Variate. Exactly which ones are currently unclear, but currently confirmed is [[Wave]]. After being formed, he found a job at [[Interdimensional Defense & Advanced Research Center|I.D.A.R.C.]], and made a few friends. He now works closely alongside another very new entity, [[Nuclide]].
