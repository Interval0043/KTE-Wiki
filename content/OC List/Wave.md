## History

### Incitement

While technically formed within [[Kingdom III]], he didn't truly make his first noteworthy appearance until [[TFCT (Crossing)]]. He mostly kept to himself as a man of strategy and one of the first researchers of the [[Hedron|Hedrons]]. At the time, he was more of a snarky nerd, tending to not understand why other entities didn't understand things the same way he did.

### Accident

During a currently classified lab incident, he sustained minor soul damage, resulting in his vision requiring glasses. It also altered his personality, pulling back the aggressiveness in favor of a much more detached and clinical perspective. While it made him a better lab partner, some empathy was lost in the exchange, making him a worse friend.

### KTE

As a result of [[Dire Will]]'s death, he was forcibly fused with other Hedrons in the making of who we know now as [[Variate]]. Still present in his mind, he often provides tactical advice, but makes no move to assist in anything emotional. He also provided a bit of his blank emotionality to counteract [[XOR]]'s often erratic and uncontrolled nature.

## Personality

Wave, while still being cooped up inside Variate, didn't learn too much about empathy in there. He sees things as they are and as literally as they can be; he helps not because he cares, but because it's expected of him in most cultures. Less risk is also better in his eyes, so he'll almost take things with the greatest chance of occurring.

He doesn't show very much emotion, because it's what he knows. While he definitely feels, he was raised in a culture which heavily frowns upon showing any emotion, leading to his seemingly unbothered nature at all times.

## Appearance

As one of Variate's forms, he appears as floating triangles of shell with a bright yellow inner gas. He has a lab coat which is also formed from his gas, albeit darker yellow, implying that his clothing is still a part of his body. The last part to note is his glasses, which are formed from Variate's shell and give him the ability to see clearly. Specifically, they have a square frame for each eye.

## Abilities

### Exacerbation

Wave can analyze and place an effect on an enemy, deepening their weakness susceptibility. For example, if someone's weak to fire, the fire would deal extra damage.

This ability is signaled by the lenses on his glasses briefly flashing yellow.

### Burning Hold

His left hand will glow red to signify this is active. While active, he can catch any projectile or magic-based attack with it to store it in his hand. The time between activation and catching is important to note, as it dictates the reliability and strength of the move.

The activation to catch window must be no longer than 3 seconds. When releasing the magic, he must prime and then release. The prime-release time is the same as the activate-catch time, giving an incentive to activating right before an attack connects. The time it takes to catch it is also inversely proportional to the strength of the counter. The longer the time taken, the weaker the return. When caught nearly immediately after activation, the released attack will be significantly stronger than what it was originally.

Note that something like this isn't going to be constantly active, as it can catch nearly anything that falls under its category. Granted, there is a Break condition, where if an attack is high enough in strength, the counter fails to initiate entirely.

### One More

Using a bit of energy and active uptime, Wave can initiate a One More, giving himself an immediate action to take. This can be used to end strings of hits, or to get himself an opening when he needs to slide in a specific attack. He can also use it to get out of enemy hit strings, however he tends to be unreliable with it and may not exit properly.
