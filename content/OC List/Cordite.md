## History

### Pre-KTE

Originally working as a Dutch teacher in [[Cryosilis]], she had a fairly normal life. Commonly, she worked with young children and studied other subjects in her free time. Known to be soft around just about everyone, she was well liked and respected in her field.

As a hobby, she used to partake in sports that were brought over from the [[Human|Humans]], primarily Tennis. She found good fun in seeing what other cultures were like, often travelling dimensions as vacation days just to see what was in store. 

### KTE

Soon after [[Dire Will]]'s death, an Earthen [[Dimensional Fragments|Fragment]] pierced her Soul and fractured it. It began swiftly replacing her vessel's cookie dough with a muted blue gas, the Binding Soul forming in tandem with her regular [[Sprinklekit]] soul. As per usual with Souls, this causes complications, as a [[Manifold]]'s soul doesn't line up correctly with that of her original one, causing problems.

It also caused a sort of amnesia, her memories blurring and losing specificity. This is how she arrived to simply calling herself "Cordite", as she felt it was a nice name to have. Because of Cryosilis having been turned into an [[Malice#Eternal Malediction|Eternal Malediction]], she gained a distaste for [[Malice]] and wishes it to be gone, but currently does not have the fighting knowledge to make it happen.

## Personality

Even with her Soul being about 20% interchanged, she's still as soft as ever towards other people, believing in Humanism. However, because of the changes to Cryosilis, she's forced to harden up on occasion and fight for what she believes in, as much as it pains her to hurt others. She convinces herself that Malice and Maledictions aren't truly sentient as to rationalize her hate for them, therefore believing they aren't inherently good creatures.

## Appearance

### Body

She still maintains about half of her vessel being of the Cookie Dough Sprinklekit, with a tan ice cream and chocolate chunks scattered abundantly throughout. However, her height is unnaturally extended by the Earthen Dimensional Fragments|Fragment attempting to forcefully form a Manifold body on top of what she already has. Her waist, part of her head and chest, and other sections of her legs, hands, and arms are a muted blue Gas, some of the chocolate chunks being suspended within it.

### Specific Bodily Oddities

- Her left ear is entirely missing.
- Her right hand is a regular Manifold hand, but the rest of her extremities are paws akin to Sprinklekit|Sprinklekits.
- Her legs and arms are elongated, giving her a much lankier sort of look. The gas adds very little weight, that being about 12 pounds.
- Her right eye is partially affected, having blue gas swirling in her sclera. Her left eye is unaffected.
- Fur doesn't cover the gas, so she just looks weird.
