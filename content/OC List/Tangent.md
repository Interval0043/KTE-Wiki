## History

Tangent doesn't have a whole lot of history, as most of his relevant time was spent existing either as an older brother to [[Acheron|Arc]] back in [[Kingdom]], or residing with [[Shara Astral]] in [[TFCT]]. 

## Personality

The best word to describe Tangent would be a perfectionist, specifically for his own actions. A lot of what he does is precise, methodical, and works quite well with [[Shara Astral|Shara]]. Although, his perfectionism tends to bleed into her work as well, finding himself obsessing over small details without quite realizing why they exist, or whether or not they're good.

Not only that, but he finds himself wanting to consistently make sure Shara is happy both with herself, him, and the relationship as a whole, so he'll be hovering around her quite often. He tends to pay very good attention to things and to not get off track, content with keeping others in line when he needs to.

## Appearance

Tangent often finds himself wearing a tactical vest with nothing else under it, exposing more slime than most of his species usually does. Otherwise, he also maintains a great pair of combat pants and boots, ones which withstand most typical combat and day-to-day use. All of them were created by Shara for the express purpose of facilitating his usual combat style.

### Vest

The combat vest looks like a standard one most military operators use, fitted with plenty of protective padding and plate slots on the front and back. See the Equipment section for more info on what exactly the plates do.

### Pants & Boots

Both the combat pants and boots are the same color, that being a dark gray. Both of them have caked-on mud that doesn't wear off of things, giving it minute dark brown splotches that add to his look. On the pants specifically, he has a quick holster for his tomahawk on his right side, allowing him to pull it and do combat when necessary.

## Relationships

First, and most important right now, is his love life with Shara. The two of them live at the [[Legion Of Nazareth|L.O.N.]] headquarters, in which Tangent doesn't do a whole lot. His perfectionism often applies to her as well, finding the best ways to do things for her and to make things work across species boundaries.

Second is his brother, [[Acheron|Arc]]. While the two haven't spoken much for quite a while, they're still siblings and have been close at one point in time.

Third was his working relationship with [[Interval]]. For a period of time, the two of them worked together and became mildly close friends, still keeping up with eachother to this very day. Whether or not it's for an ulterior motive on Interval's end is unclear, but Tangent isn't concerned on that.

## Weaponry

### Tomahawk

Tangent always keeps one tomahawk at his side at all times, and he has a maximum of one. It's made to be highly aerodynamic and to complement Tangent's expertise in predicting trajectory, as well as serve many use cases where speed and strength are both necessary.

When discussing techniques of throwing, retrieval, and everything in between, it first must be stated that Tangent is a creative man when it comes to throwing the tomahawk. As such, he's able to string together combinations of attacks, all of which do different things. The first of which is the **Rebound Blow**, one of his most common motions. It's a simple swing with his right hand, angled to bonk the enemy with the handle end of the tomahawk, returning it almost immediately to Tangent's hand thereafter. The goal of the motion is to either deal small damage or to provide a brief concussive effect for follow-up throws, depending on where the tomahawk struck.

The second notable technique is the **Backhand Slip**, where he wraps his left hand around his back before throwing the tomahawk, similar to throwing a boomerang. It functions similar to the Rebound Blow, except with significantly more force and meant to be a finisher from other attacks. Provides a much larger concussive effect if the head is hit, and a minor one if a helmet is hit.

The third and fourth are both very similar, that being the **Precise Stick** and the **Fast Drop**. Both of them are used to lodge the sharp end of the tomahawk in enemies, but how exactly they're done differs. The Precise Stick is a ranged throw, intended to break armor or render limbs damaged, while the Fast Drop is a melee-ranged attack from above, moving to slice downward and target the head of the enemy.

Finally, there's the **Sidewinder**, which is an intentionally curved throw to mix up what attacks he's doing. It has a wide range of speeds it can move at, and can even loop around the enemy and hit them from behind.

Notably, this list is not exhaustive. Tangent is a creative man with his weaponry, and as such will do what he feels necessary in the moment.

One last thing is the **Fast Draw**, where he draws his tomahawk up from the holster and slices the enemy with it. This will usually only be done before battles, or rarely during the battle. When performed before a battle to initiate one, it would give Tangent an Advantage state.

### Other

If anything else can be thrown, like a brick, a trowel, a screwdriver, or anything pointy / blunt, he will be throwing it. While it doesn't have nearly the same complexity as his tomahawk, it still functions as auxiliary damage.

## Equipment

### Armor Plates

The armor to be put into the plate carrier protect his chest and back from blows, until they break, in which case they need to be replaced manually with new ones. Specifically, the vest empties out the shards out the bottom, and a new plate is inserted at the top. One exists for each side.

More on the protection aspect, they function as physical and magic absorbers, taking damage until breaking. When a physical blow is dealt, some rebound damage may be applied, as the enemy is attacking a hard surface. This only applies if the plates don't break immediately upon hit.

His head and legs are not protected.

## Abilities

### Return

Tangent returns his tomahawk to his hand, from anywhere within reason, provided it's not destroyed.
