## History

### [[TFCT (Prequel)]]

Interval was the first of the [[Manifold|Manifolds]], his formation taking place in [[Foreau]], where TFCT (Prequel) focused on initially. He ended up joining a group of vigilantes, being the only Manifold in a group of primarily [[Sprinklekit|Sprinklekits]], and a few other species. His main concern was the thought of not being useful, as his body didn't initially lend himself to [[Magic]] like everyone else's.

All he had was his MP443 Grach and a dream, working alongside the group to not only clear Foreau, but other dimensions of a currently unspecified threat. Through this work, he became well-renowned for his tactical mind, being able to spot inconsistencies and tactical missteps swiftly.

Near the end, his Absence [[Dimensional Fragments|Fragment]] awoke and gave him the potential to utilize Void Magic, although he didn't quite understand it yet.

### [[TFCT (Crossing)]]

With his interactions among the [[Hedron|Hedrons]], he felt much more at home with the sister species. Even though he was still one of the only *known* Manifolds, he didn't feel as alone anymore and felt about as capable as everyone else, giving him a peace of mind he wouldn't have again for a long while.

With the unified threats carrying from [[Kingdom]] and TFCT (Prequel), he formed what is now the [[46th Specialized Division]], originally just known as [[46th Specialized Division|Division 46]].

### Crossover: [[Mirror]]

When Interval initially met [[Mirror]], it happened for two reasons. First was the AU Divergence, relating to how Interval lost a close ally in his line of work, whereas Mirror didn't. Second, Mirror was brought over into AUID 0 through an unknown incident at his own workplace, causing the two to cross.

Mirror was always known as the larger and softer Interval, not nearly as hardened by war. More on that is on his page. The two became close friends, with the duo having to deal with threat after threat, sometimes on their own.

### Decline

Throughout the rest of [[TFCT]], Interval didn't have a great life. His gas grew progressively darker as he had to withstand a significant amount of bullshit just to live. This includes:

- A Burn Transformation sparking the creation of [[Scorch]], and permanently putting him on [[Cosmos]]' shit list. This is very important.
- Multiple sieges on cities, causing him to be known as a warmonger by many.
- His body being used as a puppet numerous times.
- [[Malice]] causing him and all of his people to go into hiding.
- Being falsely lead by [[Alter]] and nearly being one of the accomplices in launching a quark-erasure nuclear device.
- The simple fact that he can never catch a break.

Safe to say, the guy's about as broken as they get for Manifolds. Through the years, he's moved far away from his firearm, much preferring CQB as to get his anger out.

### Safety

Interval has spent much of the last 30 years in complete isolation from human contact. For one, it's given him the ability to appreciate nature much more, and therefore intensified his hatred of Malice. Second, he's gained the ability to understand non-speaking / non-anthropomorphic animals on a deeper level, often finding solace in their non-judgmental presence.

### [[KTE (Where Life Stands)]]

Now well-rested and realizing he has work to do, he reformed [[46th Specialized Division|46SD]] and is currently making plans. With only his right arm and an unending hunger, he's ready to hunt Malice and show them who they're up against.

## Personality

Long gone is his vitriol and vengeance, now he's just really tired in an almost endearing way. Life's had a way of giving him exhaustion, and his Absence Fragment doesn't help in that regard. He very much enjoys naps, much like myself.

For a long while, especially while in isolation, he's wished for someone to spend his time with. While preferably a lover, it could even be someone he could consider a friend. He thinks of himself as being disrespected by most, which may be true considering his past.

Yet another product of his isolation is his unwillingness to talk, preferring to not speak unless asked questions directly, and will only answer as necessary. Social concepts tend to lose their hold in extended periods of being alone.

## Appearance

First and most prominently are his eyes, as they're almost always half-lidded and sagging, unlike the norm where they're completely open at almost all times. He also has a mild standing forward hunch, likely because of carrying heavy objects by himself quite often in his own living. He's thinned out a little, despite Manifolds not typically gaining or losing weight, but something weighing down on his soul has caused him to lose gas.

On to the clothes, he typically wears a tunic and leggings made of ethically-sourced animal pelt, of which is a combination of deer, elk, and a few others native to [[Foreau]]. He wears hiking boots almost everywhere, and they are not clean in the slightest, tracking a slight amount of mud everywhere he walks.
