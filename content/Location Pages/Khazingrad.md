Khazingrad is located in Dimension 36, and is one of the first and largest APRs for people to explore out there. In recent time it's undergone massive expansion, now serving as a major hotspot for new people of all kinds to interact.

## History

The city began in [[Kingdom SE]], with the arrival of the [[Hedron|Hedrons]], as much more of a rural port town, existing on the edge of a lake. While fairly run-down, it had its charm and its crime, setting a very interesting scene for [[Kepler]] and [[Poinsot]] as they navigated both a new world and new lives.

Through [[TFCT]], it fell and came back anew, as a far more urbanized area. Even still, it hasn't undergone major development and stayed fairly consistent for thousands of years.

Now, in [[Eternal Infection]], significant development has gone underway, especially with the area now being under protection from the [[Primordial Entity|Primordial Entities]].

## Old Khazingrad

Contained here are the original few locations of the urban startup. Despite it being named "Old Khazingrad", it's still present nearby the harbor and is a mainstay of the city, even if it isn't as popular anymore.

### Lakeside Bar

Run by [[Whistler]], it's a simple bar that many who frequent the area know, and yet it maintains a steady flow of people to where it's never too busy.

### Harbor

At this rate it's a shipping central, but many local docks and boating spots still exist by the lake. Everyone in the city keeps up the lake, and as such, there's very little trash or pollution near it. However, fishing is discouraged at the harbor, and is only permitted at a different body of water.

The lake is deep enough to where many aquatic species have taken hold and are in the process of developing an underwater city to complement Khazingrad.

The Harbor area is specifically meant for the local sections. For the much more industrial side of things, that would be the Shipping District.
