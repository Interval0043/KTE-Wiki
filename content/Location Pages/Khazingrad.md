Khazingrad is located in Dimension 36, and is one of the first and largest APRs for people to explore out there. In recent time it's undergone massive expansion, now serving as a major hotspot for new people of all kinds to interact.

## History

The city began in [[Kingdom SE]], with the arrival of the [[Hedron|Hedrons]], as much more of a rural port town, existing on the edge of a lake. While fairly run-down, it had its charm and its crime, setting a very interesting scene for [[Kepler]] and [[Poinsot]] as they navigated both a new world and new lives.

Through [[TFCT]], it fell and came back anew, as a far more urbanized area. Even still, it hasn't undergone major development and stayed fairly consistent for thousands of years.

Now, in [[Eternal Infection]], significant development has gone underway, especially with the area now being under protection from the [[Primordial Entity|Primordial Entities]].

## District: Old Khazingrad

Contained here are the original few locations of the urban startup. Despite it being named "Old Khazingrad", it's still present nearby the harbor and is a mainstay of the city, even if it isn't as popular anymore.

The area itself is very natural still, opting to keep many trees and greenery around, as well as significant amounts of walking and biking space. So much so, in fact, that there's no driving allowed in this district.

The commissioners took inspiration from [[Human|Humans]] quite a lot for this district, as it comes across as much more European in design philosophy, despite the city not being anywhere close to Earth.

### Lakeside Bar

Run by [[Whistler]], it's a simple bar that many who frequent the area know, and yet it maintains a steady flow of people to where it's never too busy. It maintains an exterior that is in line with the rest of the architectural standards of Khazingrad.

The inside, though, is primarily mahogany. The left side (from the bartender's perspective) is a simple wall with ample windows, giving an excellent view of the nearby lake and Harbor. On the right are some TVs, broadcasting other dimension's media, especially those from humans. Some are also broadcasting Khazingrad's development, or some news. Under the TVs are bean bags, where drunkards rest themselves after one too many drinks. The bar itself is well maintained, with a large repository of bottles and taps behind the bartender on shelves and on the wall.

The bar itself has 18 stools and is the main focal point of the location, being able to seat quite a few people up front. There are also booths and tables scattered in the center, if someone wants to order through a waiter instead.

While the bartender isn't always constant, the main one present is Whistler. The only reason he wouldn't be there is because he's sick, or injured. He's almost always seen cleaning the glasses, or drinking from the nearby coffee pot.

### Harbor

At this rate it's a shipping central, but many local docks and boating spots still exist by the lake. Everyone in the city keeps up the lake, and as such, there's very little trash or pollution near it. However, fishing is disallowed at the harbor, and is only permitted at a different body of water designated for both sporting and eating.

The lake is deep enough to where many aquatic species have taken hold and are in the process of developing an underwater city to complement Khazingrad.

The Harbor area is specifically meant for the local sections. For the much more industrial side of things, that would be the Shipping District.

### Ruins

Some of the older buildings still exist here from thousands of years ago, giving a glimpse into the past of what the city once was. Preserved as a memorial and as a place for anyone to wander around, and look at.

### Auxiliary Parking

Since there's no driving, there's a parking garage on the edge of the district, with plenty of inflow and outflow needs, and space. No cost to park, and you can stay as long as you like.
