This area has its own Region Set, as it's an interesting space that needs regions to work.

Between everything or otherwise, resides the Negative Space.

Anything destroyed in standard areas is sent here. Anything created is taken from what was put in. Dimensions that fall apart too fast and too far are sent here to be locked away, forever floating in a cacophony of nonsense.

If you can make sense of it, have at it. Find what you need. But make no mistake, the outside world will not help you. And neither will I. There's no guide this time, only you. This space is free reign, create whatever areas you want, explore what you want. You can't impose rules on the truly chaotic.

*i will remove places that objectively break what little balance we have, or otherwise necessary

Take or give, very few live here, but things may kill. Be aware.
