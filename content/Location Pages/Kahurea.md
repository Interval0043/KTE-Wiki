Kahurea is an entirely separate reality, if one can even call it that, acting as a "Mirror" of EI. While EI as a construct is composed of what exists, including what ceased to be, has been created and can be created or destroyed, and even concepts as abstract as one may be, Kahurea is composed entirely of what can not, will not, does not and should not exist.
Even fundamental concepts such as Time, Space, Matter or Existence are instead replaced by other things that can not even be called concepts.
