Species pages or concepts often have certain restrictions by the author on usage. That's where these Author Notes come in. They use Obsidian Callouts and have their terms provided within them.

> [!success] Author Note
> The Success callout is used when something is freely available and for anyone to use or suggest ideas for. This is the most lenient of them all.

> [!warning] Author Note
> The Warning callout is used when the usage of the content in the page is conditional; usually asking the author or some other condition.

> [!failure] Author Note
> The Failure callout is used when something is kept to the author and feedback is either not accepted or accepted only by close people, or the content isn't usable.
