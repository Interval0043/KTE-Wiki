## Part 1: Page Labels

Pages have labels, sometimes. You know on Wikipedia those things that appear at the top of the page that tells you some important info about the page itself? We have those, too!

When adding labels to the top of a page, they must go in this order when applicable. Important Page *must* be above Stub, which also *must* be above Developing Page.

### Important Page

Self-explanatory. Used when a page is an important concept.

Only applies to pages under Concepts.

> [!success] This page is an important page.
> It's recommended you read through it to better understand our story and server.

### Stub

Used when a page has a lack of detail, but isn't necessarily early in development. It may just be unfinished or not up to comparative quality.

> [!faq] This page is a stub.
> Consider asking the author to improve on it, or add something yourself with their permission.

#### Deliberate Stub

Used when a page is a stub, but is meant to be. Note: Stub and Deliberate Stub can only be one or the other, not both.

> [!faq] This page is a stub, but deliberately so.
> There's nothing really more to add.

### Developing Page

Used when a page isn't necessarily a stub, but is still currently being worked on and is subject to change.

> [!info] This page is currently under construction.
> Changes will be made semi-frequently.

### Unverified OC

Used when an OC is unverified.

> [!warning] Currently unverified.

### Deceased OC

Used when an OC is deceased.

> [!failure] Deceased.

## Part 2: Links

The body of a page will tend to have links to other pages. To prevent clutter, here's our official stance on links:

First, a concept, species, name, or other page should only be linked once in the main body of a page.

> [!example] Linkage Example 1.
> I really like [[Sprinklekit|Sprinklekits]].
> I love Sprinklekits so much.
> 
> Who I really don't like is [[Noite Will]].
> Noite did nothing and died. Not cool.

Second, always link concepts in a callout.
