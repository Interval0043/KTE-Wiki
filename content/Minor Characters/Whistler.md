## Appearance

Whistler's basis, and his name, come from the Windows Codename Whistler logo (seen below in the gallery).

First, the fur color is a proud white, with light coffee stains down the front of his neck fur. Second, he wears a blue collared shirt, with the top remaining a little unbuttoned, along with a green overcoat. Both of which also still have mild coffee staining, and are well-worn, fraying at some edges. Third, he wears red cargo pants with yellow accents, along with red shoes.

His eyes and mouth on the visor are white, and his hands remain uncovered.

## Personality / Mannerisms

Whistler is a very skilled bartender, often partaking in casual conversation with any of the patrons. He's a sight for sore eyes for some, an introduction to [[Khazingrad]] for others. He's very firmly spoken though, opting to engage in conflict as necessary.

He's a perpetual coffee drinker, always having a pot at the ready at all times. Despite this, he looks neither tired nor hyped up, keeping his usual neutral expression. Although, that isn't to say he's expressionless, as he's quite the opposite.

He has a soft spot for drunken people, finding them humorous and frequently watching them within his own bar, both for amusement and to make sure they don't destroy anything.
